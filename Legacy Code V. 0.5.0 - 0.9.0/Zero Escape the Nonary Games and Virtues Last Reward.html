Quick Overview

A collection of the first 2 Zero Escape games 999: 9 Hours, 9 Persons, 9 Doors and Zero Escape: Virtue's Last Reward. Once exclusive to the Nintendo DS and PlayStation Vita. 9 people are Forced into a deadly game, known as the Rabbit game as explained by Zero III, that could cost them their lives. They need to work together in teams to solve escape rooms to get the key to move on and escape the facility they are trapped in. But to what end?
