Quick Overview

Welcome to my walkthrough for Payday 2: Crimewave Edition. I will explain each trophies requirements, the difficulty trophies will be a bit different because they now stack. I will provide stealth methods and videos for easily stealthable heists. DLC Trophies are included at the bottom, they are in order of the PSN listing.

Scarface DLC is no longer available to purchase. Presumably OVERKILL has lost the license to sell this DLC at this time.
Difficulty Rating

6/10

Online/Offline Trophies

Most trophies can be done solo offline but some for sure requires a team of 4. The difficulty trophies are obviously easier with more human players but with the updated AI they aren't that bad solo.

Kingpin

The Platinum, earn all the other base game trophies.

Coming in Hot

This can be done on any difficulty but I suggest OVERKILL or below as the driver dies quicker on higher difficulties and be sure to buy the armoured escape. He appears in the street to left of where you exit the truck. You can hide under the staircase after securing the last coke bag and wait for him.

Extremely easy, on Day 2 of Watchdogs throw a bag of coke in the water. Be sure to retrieve it from the truck as it will respawn there.

Caribbean Pirate

This one is a little buggy. Cook at least 4 bags on Day 1. On Day 2 deliver the meth, find the info and your payment. Kill the guards, steal the info and secure the payment. Then steal back the meth.

Doctor Fantastic

After you go down in the lab to search the correct engine you'll have to search for 3 things which can be in the lab or in the house.

-A clipboard that tells you which gas element it is. The clipboard will look like this and will say Helium, Nitrogen or Deuterium. In the lab there's a white board that tells you the color of that specific gas canister.

-Another clipboard that tells you how many nozzles it has. This one is rather easy, on the clipboard it will say 1xH, 2xH or 3xH on the bottom. If it says 1xH it means it has 1 nozzle going to the gas canister. 2xH has 2 nozzle's and 3xH three.

-The Computer with a green graph (this one is always in the lab.) In the top right corner it will say a psi number, with an < or >.

> 5812 = bar above or equal to 401

< 5812 = bar below or equal to 401

> 5783 = bar above or equal to 399

< 5783 = bar below or equal to 399

You can find a barometer on the engines, now last step is to figure out which engine it is.

You can use Big Oil Calculator to help you narrow it down if you find the clues.

Let's Do Th...

You'll need Shaped Charges from Technician. Start Ukrainian Job on Hard, you have a 50% chance of getting the correct safe. As soon as it starts mask up run in blow open one of the safes grab the Tiara and run back to the van. If it's the wrong safe restart and try again.

Lord of War

Recommend doing this on OVERKILL or below as there are 2 Hangers on Mayhem and above. Either Bring a saw,the Enforcer, Shaped Charges or Upgraded Drill from Technician to get the trucks open.

Masked Villain

This can be done anytime, if you have the DLC you are given one of each new mask. Preferably you'll want a mask that you can Return to your stash rather then sell, like a DLC Mask or most normal and some Infamous masks. Add a mask to your inventory, press triangle to customize it, apply Plastic material, press triangle to finalize changes and confirm. You can then return the mask or sell it.

Don't Bring the Heat

Ideally you'll want 2 people for this but you can do this by yourself on normal. Ace all of Shinobi in Ghost, optionally Jack of all Trades in Engineer under Technician. Basic Forced Friendship under Mastermind is also helpful. Set ECM Jammers as your primary and body bag cases as secondary if you have Jack of all trades. Pick weapons with high concealment and add mods that boost concealment. Inner Pockets in Ghost can help. Ideally you'll want detection under 10, you can go as low as 3.

For pre-planning place a body bag case in the West stairwell, a key card in the management corridor, and vote for the elevator trick escape. Start the heist. The host should be doing the stealthing as the desync is less for them. First thing you need to do is find a key card, it will spawn in a random spot. On the counters behind the tellers, on a desk in the upstairs or downstairs offices, on the coffee table just outside the upstairs offices, on the counter or tables of the cafeteria, or on the desk of one of the mangers offices. Optionally you can look for the key box as well, it will be on the wall by the door leading to the offices behind the tellers, in one of the mangers offices, if not it's on a wall in the vault area. After picking up the keycard in casing look for the server door, it will be downstairs across from the cafeteria, beside the West stairwell or in the management corridor. Check for cameras in the stairwell or corridor, if any mask up and break one at a time to lure the 2 front guards to you. Kill them in a place you won't get caught, answer their pagers and bag their bodies if needed. There are 4 wandering managers, and lady in a red pantsuit, a lady in a blue skirt, an old lady and a man (He has a 50% chance of carrying a key card). Tie them up given an opportunity. Once both guards are down knock the painting off the safe in the office and place a drill (preferably not upgraded) once it breaks leave it alone, it's a decoy. If you have a partner they can scout and mark guards for you while in casing. Now open the server room door using an ECM Jammer, DO NOT USE THE KEYCARD. Use the 2 keycards to remove the lasers from the vault area doors. The host, who is stealthing, should sit in the server room and interact with the computer until their indicates they found the correct computer. If you are soloing you will need to check each computer one at time. They can be activated through the divider walls if you can see the yellow outline. Be careful not to get spotted. Once you get the code activate the time lock. The host should sit by the phone in the manager's office and watch the upper elevator, your partner should watch the 1st floor elevator. Answer the phone, if it goes well your good, otherwise you'll get GenSec Security doing rounds. Killing civilians increases the chances of failing the call. Bain should mention elevators moving to your floor. You will either get 2 civilians or a civilian and a guard. They both need to be dealt with like the others. If your out of cable ties feel free to kill them. Stand by the broken drill and you should hopefully see outlines of guards through the wall if they do not move they are caught by your decoy. There are 5 guards in the back area, 4 roamers and the camera man. You'll want the 4 roamers stuck in the stairwell. If you have a partner have them Scout for you by marking guards and cameras, if you don't have the keys keep an out for a white box marked GenSec. The camera room spawns on the right side at the top of a stairwell, downstairs by a reception counter, or just upstairs by the reception counter. Kill the camera man if you feel necessary. Make your way to the fingerprint scanner and activate it. Next you'll have to press 2 buttons, one on either side on the room. Take your time and be patient. Once that's done, either you or your partner has to go back to the managers office to get the code from his computer. Now open the vault. Use the keys to open the cages. Collect all the loot and throw it by the door. Now head through the middle, be careful of cameras if they are still active and guards who may not be caught yet. There is also a money counter that can pick open with Nimble Aced. Open the elevator doors and throw the bags in the open escape hatch, once you secure all the bags your home free. Congratulations.

Gage Ninja Pack
Quick Overview

The first paid DLC is a weapon pack. The trophies are fairly easy.

Difficulty Rating

2/10

Online/Offline Trophies

All trophies are possible solo offline with little difference playing in co-op.

Swiss Cheese

Can be done on any heist, but I suggest a single day heist such as Jewelry Store or Ukrainian job. Best done solo with or without AI unless you have a friend to help you. You do not have to complete the heist, it pops on the 100th kill.

Shuriken Shenanigans

This one's pretty simple, Stealth Bank Heist: Gold or Cash but be sure to only kill guards and civilians with the Shuriken throwable. I'm not sure if melee nullifies it but I'm sure firing your gun does. I also didn't use my saw just to be safe. For pre-planning place 3 spy cams and a key card. Safe places to kill the guards are the hallway behind the tellers, in the copy room and if you hide in the corner while answering the pager, when they stand near the back door. They rarely go outside or on the roof but there are skylights on Death Wish or above. If your having trouble with aiming you can use your laser sight as a marker.

Names Are for Friends so I Don't Need One

This one is a bit tricky, especially if your not used to Sniper rifles. You only have 10 shots in your clip so you have to get at least 2 kills with one shot but there's an easy way to set this up. Start up Ukrainian Job on Normal, dominate 2 cops by shouting at them with R1, if your having trouble hit them once with a weak melee weapon (R3), and try to line up a shot so that it hits them both. If you need to you can dominate eleven cops so you don't miss a shot, but only 5 can be dominated at one time on normal. Another method is, start a Cook Off on Normal make sure you have Swan Song, maybe accuracy with hip firing as well and go to the upstairs lab, wait till at least 11 cops swarm the room, let them down you and spam Triangle.

Fugu Fighter

Can be done on hard difficulty, I suggest Hoxton Breakout, look for a group of shields and let the AI distract them. Run behind them and fire as quickly as possibly. I used the plains rider bow, the crossbows I found have a slow rate of fire. Also you don't want to kill them right away.

Gage Spec Ops Pack
Quick Overview

The eleventh paid DLC. These trophies are not too challenging.

Difficulty Rating

3/10

Online/Offline Trophies

Ducking Awesome

You'll need a shotgun with the Donald's Horizontal Leveler barrel ext. Mod. Any shotgun will do. Also use 000 buckshot if you have it. You CANNOT use AP Slugs. Start Birth of Sky on normal. Go up to the catwalk and open all the doors. You should see a line of enemies gathered in either walkway eventually. Line up your shot and fire.

The Reconing

Use any gun with The Reconnaissance sight and choose any heist on very hard. You'll have to keep track on your own. Might be best to have AI off as they may steal your kills. Not sure if special kills count but as they are counted separately in the stats at the end of the heist best to assume they don't.

Celsius or Fahrenheit

45° Red-Dot Sight is a gadget and takes the place of a laser. Since this has to done on overkill I recommend the Thanatos .50 Sniper rifle, it can one shot most enemies especially if you use the tank buster barrel. Killing civvies counts as a miss, a cop getting healed by a medic counts as a miss. Choose a small map heist, I chose Ukrainian Job. Kill someone then press down the d-pad, as you'll see it swaps your sight. Do this after each kill and it will pop after the 10th kill.

Scarface Mansion Heist
Quick Overview

The twelfth paid DLC. These trophies are very challenging. Delisted.

Difficulty Rating

7/10

Online/Offline Trophies

Most trophies are possible solo offline but the speed run may be a bit too difficult solo.

Every Dog Has His Day

Like any DW heist dodge is helpful but there are turrets and snipers on this map so you may want to try an armour build. Bring along the Commando 101 Rocket Launcher as it destroys the turrets pretty easily and you can use it to get another trophy.

After hacking the computer with the usb there will 2 boxes you will be prompted to open to continue the hack. There at least 3 different locations they can be. If you see a gas can outside anywhere grab it to save time, it will not be marked out for you later. Once you get inside there will be 8 paintings you need to burn. Then you need to help Bile airlift cars outside. There are a bunch spread around the parking lot. Then you have to kill Saso's men, kill Saso and secure the loot from the floor safe.

Say Hello to My Big Friend

Pretty easy takes only a couple shots. Can be stacked with Every Dog Has His Day. You must be the host for this to pop.

Look at These Pelicans Fall

There are 6 possible locations but only 2 spawn each time. You may need a sniper rifle to see them easier. They are outlined in red.

1.Located on the beach close to the gardening shed. You have to climb a set of wooden crates to see it or stand on the balcony on the first floor of the mansion.

2.Located in the same area as Flamingo No.1.

3.Facing the gate, it can be found on the left. Two flamingos can spawn in this area.

4.Located right of your spawn location. It's right next to the driveway on the left of Flamingo No.3. Two can spawn in this area.

5.Located left of Flamingos 1 and 2. It's a bit hard to spot if you're not on the balcony on the first floor.

6.Located on a small island close to the garage next to the pier. Two flamingos can spawn in this area.

What? You Want Me to Dance?

I highly recommend a 3-4 man team to make it easier. ECM rush to clear the outside guards, be sure to bag any in front of cameras and in front of doors. Then finish the heist as normal. John Wick Heists
Quick Overview

The thirteenth paid DLC. These trophies are a bit challenging. Delisted.

Difficulty Rating

6/10

Online/Offline Trophies

Most trophies are possible solo offline but Pass the Ammo can only be done online.

Great White

This is a stealth only heist.

Thalassophobia

You will need at least 2 people to complete this. You and your partner should split up. Mics would help if one reaches the computer first you can read out where to go. Refer to Great White for a walkthrough of the heist. Though money bag positions change with each restart. It may take a lot of restarts for you to get an idea of where everything is located. Make note of food carts and lifeboats (lifeboats are the white containers and food carts are the black ones) when heading to the laptop. You may need to open on of them.

Pacifish
Vengeful Rain

You want long range weapons that are high powered as you have to protect Charon from high above, accuracy can help save ammo. There will be a lot of dozers. Doctors bags are best as you will probably go down a lot. Having a teammate with drill skills to speed up opening the cages will also help. I do not recommend dodge for this as there a number of snipers you must kill and they will down you pretty easily.

A Rendezvous With Destiny

I recommend a 2 man team for this one, though it is possible solo. I also recommend the .308 Contractor Rifle and Arbiter Launcher. One person should have drill skills, third person will focus on the cages. While the other person kills the snipers in the second building. The arbiter can be used to take out groups of 2 or more enemies. Let the AI deal with the cops. They should have no problem on overkill except for dozers. Once you get the top floor run past the Cloaker or dozer and jump the roof, drop in the right sunroof to land on the wood.

Pass the Ammo

You will a full lobby for this, 4 people with 2 ammo bags, basic or aced. After you reach the second building place all 8 inside the ammo cage after drilling it open and finish the heist, this must be done on overkill or above.
