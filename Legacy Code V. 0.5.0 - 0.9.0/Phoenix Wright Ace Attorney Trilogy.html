Lunchbox Specialist

(AA Ep. 5)



Investigation Day 1:

  1. Half and Half - Story Related
  2. Crunchy Goodness - Story Related
  3. General Tso's Trilobite - Exhaust all her dialogue options.
  4. Lunchland's Pickle Supreme - Before going to the Prosecutor's Office present your Badge to Angel.

Trial Day 2:

  1. Caviar - Story Related
  2. Fiesta- Story Related
  3. Fingerprint - Story Related
  4. Salmon Swirl - During her cross-examination present crime photo at her statement "The chief prosecutor was holding a knife in her right hand..."
  5. First Love - You will be given the option to raise an Objection, instead say No Problem.
  6. Moss Surprise - Present Goodman's Autopsy Report at her statement "Nothing else could drive that human machine to plunge the knife in again and again..."
  7. Crime Scene - Story related
  8. P'tooey - Press her statement "Ah yes. When I arrested her, she mentioned her muffler!" Choose Ask Further and press her new statement. Then present the Parking Lot Floor Plans. When given the option choose "What she saw".
  9. Orange-Peel - Follow the steps for P'tooey but instead choose "Order of events".
  10. Squid Wheels - Follow the steps for P'tooey but instead choose "Where she saw it.". Then choose "Distance to the crime".
  11. Jumbo (Triple-Deckered) - Story Related
  12. Pepper Fish Gut - During the witness statement "Decisive Evidence" press all her statements.

Investigation Day 2:

  1. Deli: This one is story related. Visit the Underground Parking Lot and meet Angel to unlock this one.
  2. Salisbury Steak: This one is story related. You need to present "Goodman's Note" and "Crime Photo" to open up more talking options. Talk to Angel using all available options. This lunchbox is required to advance the story.
