Quick Overview

This is the Remake of the 2001 classic psychological horror. It does the franchise justice other than a few unneeded changes it's a fairly well done Remake. It even retains the separate difficulty settings for combat and puzzles, with new puzzles having been added to spice things up. It's got an easy platinum as everything can be done on the lowest difficultly settings.

Nice And Cozy
Sidequest Collect.

Collect the Neely's Bar coin from the cash register in Texan Café. While exploring the south end of Martin St, you can find a matchbook on a corpse that marks Neely's Bar on your map. Inside the bar, inspect the broken jukebox to get half a record, alongside a note pointing toward Groovy Music and Saul Street Apartments. Proceed north to Groovy Music, smash the interior glass to find the other half of the record and vinyl glue, then combine them in your menu. Head to the Saul Street Apartments next to the auto parts shop. Grab the Apartment 5 key from the front desk office, head to the second floor, and navigate through Apt 5's cracked walls to reach the outside fire escape. Climb up to enter Apartment 9 on the third floor. Go to the bathroom and interact with the hole in the wall 3 times to pull out the missing Jukebox Button. Return to Neely's Bar, load the record, place the button, insert the coin, and press C2. Take the Neely's Bar Key from the machine and use it to unlock the bar's back door. Follow the linear path through the back alleys until a cutscene plays; you will find the Woodside Apartments Key inside a pile of bloody clothes on the street near a blue car shortly after.

Alone Again

Unforgivable

Inner Sanctum

This refers to Pyramids Head's room. It is a circular room and in it you will find the Great Knife which he has discarded in favour of a spear. It is slow and heavy but does a great deal of damage. Originally there were supposed to be 2, indicating it was actually a pair of giant scissors.

A Human Being

After making it through Toluca Prison you'll find Eddie but he's acting strange. He's loses his mind and attacks you with a revolver be you are "laughing at him", defend yourself. This fight can be rather difficult, even on normal, due to the fog. Dodging is essential for this fight. Dodge his shots and strike when you spot him.

  1. Have as many optional conversations with Maria as possible. Such as when Maria looking at her old outfit in Jack's Inn, when you visit the Baldwin Mansion, try to enter Silent Historical Society, explore Rosewater Park.
  2. Be sure to check in on her at Brookhaven often, there are 2 missable cutscenes. Also check on her after finding her in the Labyrinth.
  3. When doing the mirror puzzle in Lakeview Hotel place the ripe apple on the plate instead of the rotten one.
  4. When you get the rust and scarlet eggs, go through the scarlet door.
  5. Make sure to keep Maria safe from enemies.
  6. Do not inspect Mary's photo or letter or Angela's knife.
  7. Do not listen to the full conversation in the long hallway before the final boss fight.

Defy Even Death

This is only Available on New Game+. There are 4 items you must collect. Complete the game normally with all 4 in your inventory to get this ending.

  1. Crimson Ceremony: In the graveyard where you first meet Angela it it sitting in front of a lonely grave by the shore.
  2. White Chrism: In South Vale (West) after getting through the gate at Jack's Inn in can be found on a table outside the Baldwin Mansion.
  3. Obsidian Goblet: It is found in the Silent Hill Historical Society in the room with the Pyramid Painting hidden in a crevice.
  4. Lost Memories: Inside the Lakeside Hotel Lost and Found, it replaces the Lost and Found Note (collectible).

The Goodest Boi

This is only Available on New Game+. In order to get this ending you must find the 2 broken pieces of then dog key and use it on the Observation center in Lakeview Hotel. It is next to room 312.

  1. Broken key piece 1: In South Vale (East) next to Big Jay's Diner on Neely St. is the Pet Center. Head inside to find this in the back room
  2. Broken key piece 2: In South Vale (West) just aster the Baldwin Mansion on Katz St. Your find a gate with a bone on it. Go through the gate to find this piece in the doghouse.

Tinfoil Hat

This is only Available on New Game+. You must collect and use the blue gem in 4 locations.

  1. Blue Gem: In South Vale (East) the jeweler's is next to the pet center, break the window and grab it out of an open jewelry box.
  2. Saul St. Apartments: On the roof of Saul St. Apartments there is graffiti of an eye on a vent. Face it and use the blue gem to make it glow.
  3. Rosewater Park: In Rosewater Park on the pier where you meet Maria. Use the blue gem to make it glow.
  4. Lakeview Hotel: Right after disembarking use the blue gem to make it glow.
  5. Lakeview Hotel - Room 312: Use the blue gem for the final time.

This is only Available on New Game+. It is a new ending added to the remake.

At Pete's Bowl-o-Rama open the previously inaccessible safe with the code 1887. In Brookhaven Hospital Garden open the safe found in the Gazebo to find White Claudia. (Context: White Claudia is a drug that Dr. Michael Kaufmann was trafficking to tourists of Silent Hill, it causes hallucinogenic effects as well as other side effects such as mood swings, memory loss and suicidal tendencies. Nurse Lisa Garland was implied to be hooked on it in Silent Hill Origins and wel las Silent Hill (1999). The order was also using White Claudia to gain more members and working with Dr. Kaufmann. It is implied that Father Vincent is also involved in the drug trade, he is only in the order for the money, he doesn't believe in the religion. Go to room 312 and use the White Claudia then play the tape.
