Quick Overview

A spin off of the Ace Attorney series, this time you play as Miles Edgeworth on the prosecutions side of things. It has a more in depth investigation mode where you can actually walk around the crime Scene and investigate. To top it off, this is the first time the second game has ever been officially published in North America.

A Well-Worn Organizer

You will get this naturally while going for the platinum.


Doing Things My Way

During AAI1 EP. 1 you will be given a tutorial on using Logic. Simply put, connect the pieces of evidence together to better understand what took place.


Objection!

Rebuttal's work the same as cross examination in the main series. Find the contradicting statement and present the evidence that proves it.


Checkmate

A new mechanic introduced in AAI2. Miles imagines a chess board in order to calculate an opponents moves. You must answer correctly each move, including bide my time. Please see the walkthrough linked above if you need help. The tutorial introduced with Ms. Tabby Lloyd.


Everything Old is New

The HD sprites are used by default so simply start the game and it will pop.


Wright Over Here!

During AAI1 Ep. 3 leave Wild Wild West, go through the front gate of Gatewater Land and onto a bridge to see Phoenix Wright, Maya Fey and Pearl Fey boating in the distance.


Wright Over There!

During AAI2 Ep. 5 at the beginning of the episode examine the gate to your right twice.


Ladders and Step-Ladders

An ongoing joke in the series. In AAI1 Ep. 5 on the far left of open air stage with Kay Faraday. AAI2 Ep. 3 Delicia's room on the bottom right with Tyrell Badd and Eddie Fender.


Friendship... or Pity?

AAI1 Ep. 1 In the Prosecutor's Office Corridor speak to Gumshoe and select "Anything of interest?"


A Prosecutor's True Passions

During AAI1 Ep. 2 Return to First Class Passenger seats and examine the seat with the chessboard and law book.

The Blue Badger's Daddy

Promises 6 and 7...?

During AAI1 Ep. 4 make sure you present Kay Faraday with her Promise Notebook as soon possible. This is missable.


Behind Each Bench

During AAI1 Ep. 4 there is an interval where the courtroom is empty and you can examine all 3 benches.


Lang Zi Says!

During AAI1 Ep. 5 Lang Zi and Miles will examine the Primidux statue together. Examine the front and back BEFORE examining the bottom.


Fond Memories, Detective

During AAI2 Ep. 1 after Mind Chess examine the sign post next to Winston Payne.


A Winner's Education

During AAI2 Ep. 2 press every statement during Eustace's Rebuttal.


Like Father, Like Son

During AAI2 Ep. 3 as Gregory examine the Tea Pit in Tangaroa's room. Then examine it a second in the Court Record. When playing as Miles examine the Tea Pot in the Organizer.


Glasses Battle

In AAI2 Ep. 4 when investigating the Storeroom with Ema speak to her about the topic "Ema Skye".


How Could You, Sir!?

During AAI2 Ep. 5 during the final Mind chess match select "You're a total failure of a human being!" It is advised to save before doing so as it depletes your time and damages your health bar significantly.
