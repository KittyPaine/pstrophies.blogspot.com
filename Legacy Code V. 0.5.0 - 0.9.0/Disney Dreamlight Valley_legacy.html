Quick Overview

DDV is very much like Animal Crossing: New Horizons but with Disney Characters. It is no longer set to go free to play but will continue to receive free content updates as well as paid content. The platinum is slightly grindy but will come with dailyplay. Highly recommend this app on Android for daily reminders as well as collection and in game challenge tracking.

There is no autopop, but there is a workaround through cross save, and it is cross buy. In order to get both platinum's without having to do the work twice you must play online. When switching versions it will ask if you want to use Local or Cloud save. Get the required task completed on whichever version you are playing. Switch to the other version, use cloud save, and collect the task. Then switch back to the other version and use your local save and collect it again. (Use backup if necessary.)

Dreamlight Master

The Platinum.

Angler

This one isn't misleading. Simply catch 1,800 fish. Not sure if seaweed counts for this, I presume seafood counts (lobster, squid, shrimp, etc.) though they are classified differently for Dreamlight Tasks.

Chef Cook

You can cook any meal, the easiest is Fruit Salad. It uses 1 fruit and 1 coal ore. Befriend Kristoff to help him set up his stall and he sells coal ore. The easiest 5 star meal is a large seafood platter, 1 lemon and 4 seafood (oysters, etc.)

Constructor

This will take a long time and a lot of star coins. The fastest and most profitable method is growing and selling pumpkins. You can craft miracle potions to grow them instantly with your watering can. Or you can plant them in The Forgotten Lands where you buy them to get a boost to growth speed. You can also serve meals in Remy's Restaurant for a small payout while they grow.

Bestest Friend

You befriend villagers by getting their friendship to level 10. Collect flowers and give them to them, have them follow you while doing daily tasks, giving their 3 favourite items of the day, completing their friendship quests and playing scramble coin with them (requires a rift in time). You will need a lot of iron ingots and Kristoff sometimes sells them, his stock changes daily. Your other option is Vitalys mine in Sunlit Plateau. Or you can visit a Treasure Valley (find these on twitch) through multiplayer. They have also added Dream Teams where you can request resources from other players.

Generous

You will likely get this while going for Bestest Friend, it doesn't have to be their gift of the day, anything will do.

Banker

You likely get this while upgrading your house to the max level.

Gardener

The fastest growing vegetable is lettuce. Or you can use miracle potions on your watering can to save time.

Miner

You will likely get this while mining iron for various friendship quests.

Thorn Cleaner

Every time you unlock a biome it will be covered in night thorns but that won't be nearly enough. 10 Night Thorns spawn in a random biome with 20 Max per biome for each day you login, so be sure to login at least once per day.

Renovator

The trophy description is misleading the actual tasks is making friends. You make friends by helping people move into the village. For example Anna, Elsa and Olaf, they all live in Kristoff's house which you find in the Forest of Valor, that is 4 friends total towards this trophy.

Chit Chatter

This will take less time the more villagers you unlock as this is a daily discussion it can only be done once per day per villager. In order to get new villagers you need Dreamlight to unlock new biomes and realms. Some are and will be locked behind a paywall, others will be added through free content updates.

Photographer

Easy, take out your phone and spam the shoot button.

Valley's Helper

This one is misleading. You don't just have to unlock them. You must remove at least one night thorn in each biome.

Goofy's Investor

You will likely get this by investing in various items and completing quests around the valley. This one is also misleading. You simply need to reach 9 stalls repaired. Either by unlocking all 9 stalls or upgrading ones you have access to.

Duty Bound

Super easy and the best way to grind Dreamlight. Set up a stove and crafting bench next to Goofy's stall in Peaceful Meadow, do not clean up any night thorns unless it's for a task. Buy lettuces for the plant vegetable task and harvest them for the related task. For eating a simple meal you need to eat 2 star meals. The musical fish is a bass. Seafood are Shrimp, Lobster, Squid, etc. Or you can get Dreamlight Shards from Treasure Valley's on twitch. You can also request them through the Dream Team feature.
