Quick Overview

If you bought Paradise Killer because you saw stylized 2D character art standing in a quirky world, a larger-than-life cast of eccentric suspects, and a massive murder mystery, you are in the right place. It shares that same aesthetic DNA. However, instead of being trapped in a high-stress, linear visual novel, Paradise Killer drops you into a 3D open-world, vaporwave paradise with a very chill, city-pop soundtrack.

Gaining Access to the Barracks

Accessing the Barracks is a surprisingly complex task. While there are multiple structural entrances, you are strictly limited to one specific route. To get inside, you will initially ignore the Barracks area entirely and instead target the roof of the Dead Zone. Reaching this area requires precise movement, the Visions of Cosmos and Visions of Pyramids Starlight upgrades, and both the Double Jump and Air Dash abilities.

Phase 1: Reaching the Dead Zone Roof

One of the most reliable routes to the Dead Zone roof begins at The Opulent Ziggurat save point:

  1. Facing the save point, walk forward and to the right to reach the outer perimeter of the area.
  2. Turn left and trace the perimeter until you spot a Silent Goat statue near the far back corner.
  3. Drop off the ledge directly to the right of the statue to land on the hillside below.
  4. Head toward the next tall hill right in front of you, skirting around its right flank to scale it to the top.
  5. Once at the peak, turn toward the Idle Lands tower, build up a sprint, and leap across the gap onto the lower tier of the Dead Zone roof.
  6. Move along the right side of this tier to locate an electrical box. Flip the switch to restore power, then move forward to find a ladder leading to the highest roof level.

Phase 2: Unlocking the Pathway

  1. Head through the newly powered electric gate and turn right to find a Nightmare Computer.
  2. Clear the puzzle sequence here to trigger a shift in the environment's corruption levels.

Phase 3: Infiltrating the Barracks

  1. Fast travel to the Outside Courthouse save point. Facing the save point, turn completely around and head up the concrete ramp.
  2. Vault over the ledge directly ahead to drop onto the roof below.
  3. From this grassy roof section, sprint forward and execute a chained Double Jump and Air Dash to span the gap to the taller rooftop ahead.
  4. Continue forward and slightly to the left to scale up to the absolute highest tier of the Barracks roof.
  5. Once on the top tier, push left toward the far end of the roof structure to locate an access hatch locked by a final Nightmare Computer. Bypassing this security system will permanently open the hatch, giving you full entry to the Barracks.

Accelerated Justice

Speedrun Trophy Guide (Under 10 Minutes)

This is arguably the most brutal trial in the entire trophy list, requiring you to hit the credits in under 10 minutes. Luckily, you are permitted to completely skip the investigation phase—zero evidence gathering required. Even without detective work, the clock is an unforgiving enemy due to unskippable animations, movement speed limitations, and massive walls of text.

Speedrunning Strategies

  • Maximum Velocity: Keep your finger pinned to the sprint button at all times. Never walk. Optimize your routing lines to avoid hitting geometry or getting stuck on collision boundaries.
  • Dialogue Mashing: Spam your dialogue advance inputs relentlessly. Treat every conversation like a hyper-fast quick-time event to shave precious seconds off the clock.

The Speedrun Route

  1. The Awakening: Dash out of your exile apartment, snatch up your Starlight device, and solve the security protocol sequence on the Nightmare Computer as fast as humanly possible.
  2. The Transit: Sprint directly down to the Paradise Gates area to rendezvous with Lydia. Skipping any pleasantries, hop into her transport; she will drop you off right inside the Court House steps, positioned directly in front of Judge.
  3. Triggering the Trial: Let Judge brief you on the slaughter of the Council. The absolute second the dialogue frees your character, immediately turn around, sprint back, and open the main doors leading out to the island. This action officially flags the trial phase as active.
  4. The Execution: Spin back around, talk to Judge, and initiate the court proceedings. Mash through the legal dialogue blocks. With zero evidence pinning the blame elsewhere, the trial will conclude with you sentencing and executing Henry for the Council mass murder.
  5. The Great Escape: The moment the courtroom sequence ends, sprint out of the building and cross the island terrain back to Lydia's position. Interact with her to be escorted through the Paradise Gates.

Once you clear the transition, the trophy is yours. Mission accomplished!
