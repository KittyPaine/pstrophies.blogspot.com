Quick Overview

60 Seconds! is a resource management/survival game. Most of the trophies are heavily RNG based.

Reatomized is the graphically updated version of the game with bonus content added. Reatomized is mostly the same list with some added trophies for added content. For the added Reatomized trophies please see below the main list.

Note: 60 Seconds was delisted and replaced with Reatomized version.
Difficulty Rating

3/10

Online/Offline Trophies

All trophies are possible offline.

Survival Tips

Give food and water every 5 days to family members in the shelter. Give food right away to a family member who has returned from scavenging. When scavenging early on send them out with a gas mask if you have one to prevent radiation poisoning.

Collect. Grind
Danger Zone

All breakable supplies must be broken in the shelter at the time same. (gas mask, rifle, first aid kit, radio, map and flashlight). Start Apocalypse on Tsar Bomba, grabbed all the required items, Dolores, the suitcase and as much food and water as possible. If you can grab the axe and bug spray it might help you survive long enough. Wait till day 4, as you usually get an event and chance to break something by then, then start by having Dolores take the suitcase and 3 items out with her. There is a high chance of her breaking most if not all items upon her return. Keep the first aid kit in the shelter in case sickness/injury may occur. I recommend taking the flashlight, rifle and gas mask/radio. As they are the hardest to break. Sometimes you get lucky and a natural disaster will happen, simply don't save anything. Characters who are crazy will eventually break an item related to them but this is unreliable.

Sidequest Stackable Grind
Liberation

This is a very RNG based trophy and will likely be the last trophy you get. Keep scavenging until raiders take over your neighborhood. Send out an unarmed family member to scavenge. There is a chance they will be captured. Once captured you'll need to wait for a random event to occur where you attack the nearby bandit camp, with either an axe or rifle. There are a few outcomes, one of which is saving your captured family member.
