Banshee Discovered

Evidence: Ultraviolet, Ghost Orb and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: It targets 1 player to stalk/hunt. It also has a chance to use a unique scream on the parabolic microphone.


Demon Discovered

Evidence: Ultraviolet, Ghost Writing and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: It's special ability allows it to hunt above 70% sanity. Smudge is less effective, can hunt again after 60 secs instead of 90 secs.


Deogen Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Writing and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: The Deogen always knows where you are, you cannot hide from it. It walks at 3 m/s if 6m or more from the player and slows down to 0.4 m/s when 2.5 m/s from the player. It also has a 33% chance to breathe heavily into the spirit box.


Goryo Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Ultraviolet and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: A Goryo will only show itself on dots on video camera when no one is in the room. It cannot change it's favourite room.


Hantu Discovered

Evidence: Ultraviolet, Ghost Orb and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: It moves faster in lower temperatures. When the breaker is off it emits freezing breath.


Jinn Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Ultraviolet and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Cannot turn the fuse box off directly, can only turn on lights to blow the fuse box.


Mare Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Orb and Ghost Writing

Unique Behaviour: It does not like lights. Has a higher chance to perform light breaking event vs other events. Hunts at a higher sanity in an unlit room and hunts at a lower sanity in a lit room. May turn off a light switch immediately after the player turns it on.

Moroi Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Writing and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Moves faster the lower the average team sanity is. Can curse a player who activates the spirit box/hears Whispers through a parabolic microphone, causing sanity to drain faster than normal.


Myling Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Ultraviolet and Ghost Writing

Unique Behaviour: Is quieter during hunts and produces Paranormal sounds more frequently. If your flashlight starts blinking before you hear footsteps it's a Myling.


Obake Discovered

EMF 5, Ultraviolet and Ghost Orb

Unique Behaviour: Has a 16.7% chance to leave a 6 fingered hand print. Prints disappear faster than normal. Has a chance to shape-shift during a hunt, changing position or even model.


Oni Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Freezing Temperatures and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Is more active when players are nearby. Cannot produce "Airball" Ghost Event, Puff of smoke/white cloud moves towards the player and makes a sound as it disappears. Blinks less than normal during a hunt.


Onryo Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Orb and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Will attempt to hunt after blowing out 3 sources of flames, fireplaces, candles, campfire, etc. Will prioritize blowing out a candle when attempting to hunt even if a crucifix is nearby. Cannot light sources of flames, campfires, fireplaces, etc. Blows out flame sources more often the more dead players there are.


Phantom Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ultraviolet and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Is invisible in ghost photos. Is mostly invisible during a hunt and disappears when a photo is taken.


Poltergeist Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ultraviolet and Ghost Writing

Unique Behaviour: Can throw multiple objects at once. Can throw a single object with more force than a normal Ghost.


Raiju Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Ghost Orb and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Moves faster when close to active electronics, flashlight, EMF, etc. Can hunt at 65% sanity when there is at least 1 active electronic nearby instead of 50%.


Revenant Discovered

Evidence: Ghost Orb, Ghost Writing and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Is the slowest Ghost in the game, besides the Deogen, walks at 1 m/s until detecting a player by sound, electronics or line of sight and speeds up to 3 m/s. It retains the speed until it reaches the place it last detected the player.


Shade Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Ghost Writing and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Is less active with players nearby, will not hunt when players are nearby. Produces hissing and shadow form events more often than other events. Will not give evidence if a player is in the room. Shade's cannot perform singing events.


Spirit Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Spirit Box and Ghost Writing

Unique Behaviour: Smudge prevents hunting for 180 secs instead of 90 secs.


Thaye Discovered

Evidence: Ghost Orb, Ghost Writing and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: It ages when a player is nearby. As it ages it gets slower. You can use a Ouija Board to ask it's age to determine if it's a Thaye, if it's age increases it's a Thaye.


The Mimic Discovered

Evidence: Ghost Orb, Ultraviolet, Spirit Box and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: It will always present a fake ghost orb even on no/hidden evidence. It mimics other ghost abilities, changing after a brief period but cannot change mid-hunt.


The Twins Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Spirit Box and Freezing Temperatures

Unique Behaviour: Can perform 2 interactions at the same time. Can hunt from outside the ghost room. 1 twin is 10% faster than normal ghosts, the other is 10% slower than normal ghosts.


Wraith Discovered

Evidence: EMF 5, Spirit Box and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Cannot step in Salt. Can teleport to a player outside of hunts/events and can be detected with a motion detector. Place a motion detector on top of salt. If the motion detector goes off but the salt doesn't get disturbed, you have a Wraith


Yokai Discovered

Evidence: Spirit Box, Ghost Orb and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Talking near it temporarily increases its hunt threshold from 50% to 80%. When hunting it can only detect voices/electronics at a shorter range than normal.


Yurei Discovered

Evidence: Ghost Orb, Freezing Temperatures and D.O.T.S.

Unique Behaviour: Is the only Ghost that can close the exit door/door to the ghost room outside of a hunt/event. If a player is nearby some sanity is drained. Will be temporarily confined to the ghost room after being smudged. They also cannot appear in dots for 90 secs after being smudged.

Doomslayed

Set up a custom game, 0 set up time, 0 grace period, infinite sprint, with monkey paw on Tanglewood. Grab the parabolic microphone so you can track the time. Enter and sprint to the dining room, grab the monkey's from the cabinet and wish for Activity. This doubles the chances of the ghost using its a ability. Watch the timer on the Pyramid of you don't get killed within 1 minute leave the game and try again until it pops at the results screen.

Gold Hunter

Now this tricky to do as there no evidence and 0 sanity. You can change hunt length to medium without affecting the multiplier. You'll have rely on audio and visual clues. See the cheat sheet for sound clips to help identify the ghost. Be sure you set the ghost speed to 150%. A lot of people try to get a Deogen as it's slow when 2.5 m/s from the player but I've been killed twice by a Deo in my attempts so my strategy is to get a very fast ghost or a very slow ghost, compare it's walk speed to the cheat sheet take a wild guess, complete all the objectives get a Pic and hope for the best. The easiest way to find the ghost is the tier 3 parabolic microphone. Reset until you get Escape the ghost during a hunt, get a ghost Pic, and or Incense objs. Grab a paramic, camera, tier 1 doesn't attract the ghost but takes longer to take another photo, tier 3 firelight, as you'll need it get a ghost photo since it won't work if its dark, Incense and lighter, I prefer tier 3 as it freezes the ghost in place making it easier to get a photo. Optionally you can use the tier 3 headgear to see easier but it will attract the ghosts attention. Use the paramic to locate the ghost listening closely to its footsteps, hoping for very fast or very slow to help narrow down your options. Use visual clues during the hunt to help identify the ghost. Take a pic and complete your objectives as quickly and safely as possible. Then once it's not hunting make your way for the exit. Congratulations if you get it right. May take several attempts. If you get a normal speed ghost leave through the menu so you don't lose your gear, unless you can identify it with visual clues.
