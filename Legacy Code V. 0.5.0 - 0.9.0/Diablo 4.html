Quick Overview

This is the 4th entry in the Diablo franchise. It's a top down dungeon crawling fantasy game.

Difficulty Rating

7/10

Online/Offline Trophies

This game is online only. You must be connected to Blizzard's servers in order to play.


Arise Champion

The Platinum Trophy


Emancipation

There are 8 acts in total and ranges from 8-12 hours in length.


Dedicated Protector

This is a given as you need to reach 60 to unlock paragon and reach 50 as a hardcore character.


Legion Killer

You find demons just about everywhere but a good place to farm them is in the hell tide which changes locations frequently.


Undead Undone

There are number of subcategories of zombies including ghoul, shambling Corpse, etc. You'll found them mostly in Fractured peaks and dry steppes.


Exterminator

You can find the Nagari in the Swamp regions of Hawezar. Spiders are mostly found in caves.


Turned

You can find Drowned all over Scosglen.


Tortured Souls

Bandits are often found in Dry Steppes.


Hammer Down

War Cry is the earliest skill you can get that allows you to go berserk. It is in the 4th section of your skills.


Shifty Swipes

Unlock Maul and Shred to take those respective forms.


Army of Bones

You'll likely get this if you use my Necro build for hardcore. But otherwise you simply press triangle to summon your skeletal warriors/mages.


In and Out

Simply use daggers and bows/crossbows.


Master of the Elements

You can have fun with this one. Experiment with the elements.


Convenient Crafts

Elixirs are unlocked at level 10 and Incense at level 50 at the Alchemist, most cities have one.


Potent Alterations

In order to temper you need a rare or legendary piece of equipment. Then go to any blacksmith.


Living Nightmare

To unlock torment 2 you need to reach level 60 with any character and complete the main campaign. Then you need to unlock the pit by collecting 3 artificer stones. Complete tier 20, then tier 35. The Pit is located in Cerrigar. Then complete The Cathedral of Light. Move to Expert difficulty and unlock the Tree of Whispers, then complete 10 Grim Favours for your first cache which will contain your very first Nightmare Sigil. Change to torment 2 then use the sigil and complete the dungeon.


Turning the Tides

The Hell tide is a specific area of the map that is marked red by the Hell tide. Enter this area and kill enemies to collect Aberrant Cinders which can then be used to unlock hell tide exclusive chests. These cinders disappear when the hell tide ends and relocates.


Chaotic Whispers

These caches are extremely rare and will take a long time if done legitimately. However there is an exploit you can use. Drop the cache repeatedly and pick it up. This counts towards the trophy. As far as I know this has not been patched.


First Aid

The max potion is Superior Healing potion at level 60, you upgrade it at any Alchemist. You get an upgrade every 10 levels.


Master Combatant

There is a designated PVP zone located in Denshar. It is best to just boost this as it's unlikely that you'll find other players here.


Worldly Slayer

The world boss spawns every 3 and a half hours in 1 of 5 designated locations which will be marked on the map.


Estuary Sightseer

Basically uncover the whole map. Investing in movement abilities will help besides using your mount.


Curious Collector

You get Aspects from salvaging legendary equipment or completing certain dungeons.


True Perseverance

Feel free to use my recommended build.


Devoted Protector

Pick your favourite class and get them to 60 to unlock paragon. Then grind Hell Tides/The Pit/Infernal Hordes. In order to be efficient it is recommended to speed run the highest tier in the pit that you can complete in 1 and half to 2 mins.


End of the first Mother

This is the most difficult trophy. Lilith is unlocked on Torment 1. Go to Echo of Lilith. She can now be OHKO'd if your DPS is high enough.

Quick Overview

A continuation of the main campaign. It is far easier than the base game just super grindy.

Difficulty Rating

6/10

Online/Offline Trophies

This game is online only. You must be connected to Blizzard's servers in order to play.


Hatred Subdued

Complete the campaign with a a new or existing character.


Wildland Warrior

They are found across all of Nahantu.


Nahantu Sightseer

In order to explore the entire map you need to complete special side quests as well as the strongholds. Tenets of Depravity in Kurast Bazaar. Following Faith in Seven Stones, look a Akarat's Letter hidden behind a plant. The Cat-Eye Curse drops from a random corpse in a dungeon in Nahantu. Everything Old Line in Samuk.


Tormented Massacre

It will take a ton of grinding and a really good build in order to survive on torment 4.


Hireling Commander

During the main campaign you will meet Raheir and unlock The Den. From there you can accept side quests to unlock the remaining mercenaries. If you do not get them to X before the season ends they may be boosted to X by the patch. Thankfully the trophy is attached to in game challenges and will pop once all 4 are completed. Simple level them all up once and it will pop, no need to start over.


Bane of the Khazra

This is a 3 part co-op campaign. You can try the in game party finder otherwise you will need to find a partner.


Kurast Cleanser

You must complete the campaign to unlock Reunion. It has you doing tasks for the King of Kurast and his Vizier.


Devout Champion

Please refer to Devoted Protector.


Infernal Jungle

Please refer to Turning the Tides.

Fishing is a minigame requiring a button press to catch the fish when they bite, the better your timing the better your chance at a rarer fish. Look for bubbles these also have a chance for rare fish. Sometimes changing the spot you are fishing at can improve your luck. Fishing is done using the emote wheel.

Wholly Horadric

Farm for the Slaughter or Mastery set on Torment 1. For the charm you need 1 Ancestral Unique from a World Boss and 3 unique charms. To get a legendary seal run Kurast Undercity with a Tribute on Torment 1 or higher.
