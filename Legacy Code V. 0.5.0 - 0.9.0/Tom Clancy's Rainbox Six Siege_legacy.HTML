Quick Overview

Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege is a tactical pvp first-person shooter.

Important: Trophies can only be in Quick Match, Unranked and Ranked. It is supposed to be a full autopop but I've found that some trophies may not autopop and have to be redone. Progress does carry over between versions, ex. you get 5/10 frag kills on PS4, the tracker on PS5 will reflect that progress should you switch versions.
Difficulty Rating

6/10

Online/Offline Trophies

This is an online only game.

Beginner's tips: Utilize Training mode. From the main menu select playlists press R2 to access Training. The tutorials are here as well as other training modes. I highly recommend Clear House, settings are preferential. I use pre-destruction off, advanced AI, attacker. And I play as IQ to help locate the default cameras. There is a mini-map. I use it for my initial runs to learn the map layout but then do not let myself use it on sequential attempts. For practice with guns play Deathmatch, it's FFA with unlimited respawns. For attachments I use Holo B for 1x Site and Magnified A for scoped but this is preference really. For guns with horizontal recoil (left and/or right right patterns) I recommend Compensator and Straight grip, for vertical use the flash hider if not the muzzle break, and for single shot always use muzzle break, only put lasers on shotguns and remember enemies can see your laser dot. Also I use Reflex B for shotguns, except for the Supernova which I use Holo B and the TSG I use Magnified A. The Supernova and C75auto can be suppressed. In regards to sensitivity that is entirely up to you. Personally I used the shooting range and Google Gemini's help to optimize the ratio between my muscle memory and sensitivity settings.

Meat Wall

One of the toughest trophies in the game, but it can be approached with a few tactical methods. You may even unlock it completely by accident (note: Thermite's Exothermic Hard Breach charge does count toward this trophy).

    The Active Meta (The Defuser Trap): Plant the defuser directly next to a soft, breachable surface. Deploy a drone to watch the defuser, run to the opposite side of the soft wall, and plant your Breaching Charge. Monitor your drone camera; the moment a defender locks into the long disarm animation, detonate the charge. The DBNO Method: Pick Thatcher with the M590A1, down a defender with a shotgun on a wooden floor and place a breach charge next to them, then set it off.
    Warning on the DBNO Method: The classic "Down But Not Out" method is highly glitchy and unreliable under current live-service patch rules. If you choose to attempt it, the Breaching Charge must deal the definitive, final lethal blow. If the blast wave merely downs the enemy a second time instead of killing them outright, you must let them bleed out entirely—if you finish them with a weapon, the server script will attribute the kill to your firearm and the trophy will not pop. Can confirm it is working as of May 26, 2026.

Boomshakalaka

Easier said than done, even in Quick Match. People get very competitive in this mode. Just do your best. If you have the defuser, try not to rush in right away. When you're close to a bomb, drone the room and if it's clear, try to find a spot in a doorway or by a window, basically an open area that's easy to defend and difficult for a defender to hide in. If they fail to stop the defuser then attackers win. Camera ops such as Valkyrie or Echo are useful defenders, as they can place a camera in each bomb room. Echo can use his yokai to stop attackers from planting, his sonic burst stops the planting process. Try to place Valkyrie's black eyes so they can see the bomb site and aren't blocked by obstructions such as shelves and fans. The third should be placed in a nearby room or near a heavy traffic area. Having a camera in the bomb rooms can be important, because if the defuser is planted, a camera can detect it and an icon will appear. To disarm it, go to it and hold Square.
