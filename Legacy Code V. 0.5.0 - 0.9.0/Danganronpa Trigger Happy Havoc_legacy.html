Quick Overview

The Danganronpa series are detective games. Students are being murdered and you need to find out who did it or face execution. There's also a psychotic talking bear that wants you to kill your friends. This game takes place in Hope's Peak Academy.

Nine Coins, Nine Purses, Nine Bears

You get coins upon completing a chapter, more for completing hard. You can also find Coins by interacting with objects in rooms. You must have 999 at once, so don't spend any in the School Store until this trophy pops. You can use Celestia's raise skill to speed this up, you get it from her 3rd free time event. You also get 200 for completing School Mode.
