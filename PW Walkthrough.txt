Head for the Lab

Enemy 3 is just to the left of enemy 2 prone on the platform by a log. | A3S: 3E, 1 POW On the platform by the large tree. Follow the path and you should she him straight ahead sitting by a large tree and the last enemy is also by a tree just past enemy 2. The POW is at the far end of the river. | A3N: 2E There are 2 enemies wearing ghillie suits in the ruins. | A4: 4E Behind a large tree straight ahead. There are 2 walls just ahead he is behind the wall on the left.  Enemy 3 is a sniper, stand by the nearby exterior wall he is straight ahead by a damaged wall. To spot him easier consider bringing the M. Nagant but unless it's Rank 5 you must use the Mk. 22 to knock him out other wise you risk alerting Enemy 4. Enemy 4 is straight ahead from your current position prone next to the large base of a tree, you can use the M.
