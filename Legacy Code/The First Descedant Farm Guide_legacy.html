Crystallization Catalyst Blueprint

Step 1: Speed run Kingston - Grand Square - Slumber Valley (Normal) (Infiltration Mission) Optimally with Bunny. Farm maybe 10 times just in case.

Step 2: Intercept Battle Grave Walker (Normal) 3s with lv 100 gun. Open Amorphous Material Pattern 003 for 15% chance at Crystallization Catalyst blueprint.

Murky Energy Residue x8

Sterile Land - Restricted Zone - The Forgottenese (Normal) (Infiltration Mission). This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar).

Macro Molecule Gel x22

Echo Swamp - Muskeg Swamp - Vulgus Strategic Outpost (Normal). This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar) that spawns after destroying the power units.

Mixed Energy Residue x8

You get a fair amount of these from Encrypted Boxes in Agna Desert, best done with Enzo as he makes the QTE easier and gets extra material.

Farm Analyzers/Breakers on Sterile Land - Rockfall - Anticipated Ambush Point with Bunny.

Advanced Neural Circuit x18

You can get these from almost any mission is White Night gulch. But I prefer The Mountaintops - Triumphal Arch as it's a quick execution mission. This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar).

Energy Activator Blueprint

Step 1: Farm Kingston - The Fallen Theater - Magister Lab optimally with Bunny. At least 10 times.

Step 2: Intercept Boss Grave Walker (Normal) 3s with lv 100 gun. Open Amorphous Material Pattern 002 for a 3% chance at Energy Activator Blueprint.

Low Carbon Activator x55

Kingston - Fallen Theater - Vulgus Strategic Outpost (Normal). This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar) that spawns after destroying the power units.

Conductive Metallic Foil x25

You get a fair amount of these from Encrypted Boxes in Vespers, best done with Enzo as he makes the QTE easier and gets extra material.

Farm Analyzers/Breakers on Sterile Land - Rockfall - Anticipated Ambush Point with Bunny.

Heat Plasma Battery x55

The best mission to get this from is Hagios - The Corruption Zone - The Haven. This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar) about halfway through the dungeon.

Polyatomic Ion Particle x72

The best mission to get this from is Fortress - Frozen Valley - Munitions Loading Dock (Normal). This drops from an elite monster (yellow health bar).
