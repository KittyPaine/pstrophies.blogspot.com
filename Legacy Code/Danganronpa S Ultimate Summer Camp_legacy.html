Strategies for Reaching Floor 200

1. Preparation & Build Strategy

  • Prioritize High-Rarity Cards: Use SSR and U-Rank cards for your primary Tower team to take advantage of superior base stats and higher potential caps.
  • Optimize Development: Use Monobeast scrolls to access higher-level islands in the board game. More advanced squares lead to better talent fragments and stat boosts.
  • Role Specialization: Create balanced teams of four:
    • Tank: Focus on Defense and Fortitude stats.
    • Healer: Essential to have Recover Influence and Fighting Spirit at level 10.
    • DPS: High-magic/strength units with Critical Up and Skill Up talents.

2. Essential Combat Skills

  • Electric Barrier: A mandatory skill for the endgame. It significantly mitigates damage from high-level boss encounters.
  • Status Effects: Don't ignore Poison or debuffs; they are vital for whittling down high-health boss HP bars in the late-game floors.
  • Skill Mastery: Ensure your primary healing and tanking skills are leveled up to 10 via talent fragments during the 50-day development cycle.

3. Efficiency & Growth

  • Gear Crafting: Regularly craft equipment using materials found in the Tower. Focus on gear that multiplies your strongest stats.
  • The Almighty Jabberwock: If struggling, complete the requirements for the secret boss at the Ancient Temple. Defeating it boosts your character to Level 99 instantly.
  • Coin Management: If you hit a difficulty spike, backtrack to Floor 2–10 to farm Monocoins efficiently. Use these to pull for better cards rather than banging your head against a wall.

Defeated Monobeast (Tiger)!

Use the Tiger Scroll. Focus on physical damage. It acts as your introduction to boss mechanics.

Defeated Monobeast (Snake)!

Use the Snake Scroll. This one is faster; ensure your Agility stat is boosted through blue squares on Island 2 before challenging.

Defeated Monobeast (Bird)!

Use the Bird Scroll. It has higher evasion; focus on skills that increase your Hit rate or rely on guaranteed-hit talents.

Defeated Monobeast (Horse)!

Use the Horse Scroll. It hits hard. Prioritize defensive buffs or a character with high Fortitude to avoid being one-shot.

Defeated Monobeast (Human)!

Use the Human Scroll. The final challenge; bring your best-developed character with a full complement of level 10 skills.
