Quick Overview

The First Descendant is a free to play 3rd person shooter. It follows The Descendant whom is helping the inhabitants of Albion to collect the Iron Hearts and fight against the Vulgus. An alien cyborg species that are also after the Iron Hearts. The gameplay is very similar in vein to Warframe in which you play coop missions with up to 4 players and crafted items can take hours or be sped up with premium currency.

In order to grapple a weak point you need to first damage it to the point were it flashes yellow. To find weak point press R3. Once it is yellow aim at and press R1 to grapple on to the Colossi, it is very finicky keep trying. Once attached mash R2 to destroy the weak point. Do this Twice.
