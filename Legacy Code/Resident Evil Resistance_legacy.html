Quick Overview

Resident Evil Resistance is a spin off 1 v 4 pvp game bundled with Resident Evil 3 REmake. It is possible to boost this game just not easy. Matchmaking is region based. If you quit too many games in a row there is a penalty. You could try messaging the other players as well.

Difficulty Rating

5/10

Online/Offline Trophies

This is an online only game.

Mastermind Trophies
You Cannot Escape From Death

You shouldn't have to use Nicholai as he is DLC.

Annette has a discount on zombies. Her ultimate is William Birkin.

Alex has infectious creatures and specializes in traps. Her ultimate is Yateveyo (Giant plant).

Daniel specializes is puppetry aka controlling zombies. His ultimate is Mr. X aka Tyrant.

Spencer specializes in deception and slowdown. His ultimate is the disintegration field.

That's Not A Gun, This Is A Gun

Nicholai specializes in gun camera's so you may want to to play as him for this.

Can I Offer You a Cough Drop?

Easy enough as Alex with her infectious creatures and traps.

Premature Annihilation

Fill a small room, preferably containing a puzzle piece or the security guard zombie, with a bunch of zombies and use a tether/buffs. Feel free to throw some traps in there too.

Look At Me-I'm the Mastermind Now

Simply complete the mastermind tutorial.

Powerbuff Girl

Rank up Annette to rank 19 to unlocks Concentrated Enhancer. This buff is only available in area 3. Spawn a zombie far away from the currently active biocore, buff it and control it to move it to safety as needed.

I Work Out

You get three shots per buff and each shot can hit multiple creatures that are close enough together.

Will of the G

Overkill is a 1 kit KO if uninterrupted, activated with triangle, you will grab and crush the skull of a nearby survivor.

Minesweeper

Defeated means to be in a downed state, not dead. If you stack multiple traps next to each other it can down a low health survivor. Using air traps can also knock over a survivor and you can then spawn another trap next to them while they are vulnerable.

Work Hard Plague Hard

You get three shots per buff and each shot can hit multiple creatures that are close enough together.

When the Explosion is Just Right

Your best chance is to use your goo shot to pull in a survivor that has red health before self destructing.

Time's Up, Let's Do This!

Your disintegration field doesn't last that long so wait until overtime is about to end before deploying it.

Power Overwhelming

Go afk for awhile to earn enough bio energy and wait 2 minutes.

Deal With It

Try to stock up 10 bio energy and 10 skill cards costing 0-2 bioenergy then quickly use them.

Well That Escalated Quickly

Try to use tough zombies are they have more health. Moving and lunging a lot will make it difficult for survivors to shoot you.

Weird Flex but OK

Tough and partially Armoured Zombies cost 4 bioenergy each.

One Punch

This is one of Tyrants attacks. Holding circle does a charge attack that damages any survivors he hits, upon release he sends a powerful punch.

Payback Time

This is probably easiest with Alex as Yateveyo can block the final exit and the goo around it's body slows movement.

Put that Back Where It Came From

On the left side of the HUD it shows a puzzle piece on the portrait of who is currently carrying one.

Thou Shall Not Pass

Can be done with any mastermind, just focus on locking doors.

Hello Darkness My Old Friend

There are light switches on the walls in various spots of the maps.

The Best Defense is a Good Offense

Daniel is the best candidate for this, adding a few buffs may help as well.

Overtime is when the timer reaches 0, there is a bar around the timer counting down.

Survivor Trophies
Gotta Go Fast

Easy Enough with a coordinated team against a low rank mastermind.

Lab Rat

Simply complete the survivor tutorial.

Blocked

This is easiest with January. Use her personal skill to knock out a camera then shootout a second one.

Gotta Get That Boom Boom Boom

This can be done with grenades, a rocket launcher or Jill's S.T.A.R.S. Armory Rocket Launcher.

Sharing is Caring

Best done with a team of at least 3. Having a January with Cyber Monday Arsenal will make this way easier.

Weapons Don't Grow on Trees

This can be done with any survivor. Buy 2x4s or find them scattered around the map.

Meddling Kids

Your best chance is against Alex as Yateveyo is stationary and weak to fire. Bring Valerie with Explosive Knowledge Counter Attack for infinite grenades/molotov's for purchase, January with Cyber Monday Arsenal for purchasable special weapons, Becca and Jill with S.T.A.R.S. Armory Rocket Launcher. You can try to match up to boost this or message the mastermind. Or if you have a mic try asking for help before the match starts.

Get Back to Work

Easy enough with Valerie thanks to her modified first aid spray.

Eat This

Get Valerie to rank 17 and equip this skill. When you are grabbed by an enemy, if you have a grenade in your inventory, you will be prompted to press X.

Putting the Team Back Together

Best combined with Get Back to Work.

ICU

Jan Shot First

Over Watched

Kneel Before Your Queen

Guns N' Powder

Destruction 100

Bet You Didn't See That Coming

Does This Spark Joy?

Y'all Got Any More of Them Traps?

You Are Filled With Determination

Two Enemies One Kick

Can't Touch This

This one is gonna pretty difficult unless you get a camera/trap focused mastermind. I suggest buying a gun from the armory instead of using melee.

My Iron Body is Invincible

Activate his special with L2 + R2 then immediately deactivate it. Kill a few enemies and should be fully charged again so reactivate it.

Omae Wa Mou Shindeiru

Buy a baseball bat and try to kill a group of enemies as fast as possible.

I Need Healing

Start by dropping the 1 green herb you start with, after that don't pick up any healing items and do your best to survive.
