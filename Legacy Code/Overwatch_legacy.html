Quick Overview

The successor to the original Overwatch. It has gone free to play. Cosmetics can be purchased similar to Fortnite. There is also a battle pass. Thankfully they stopped adding trophies with Lifeweaver.

Boosting is possible with 10 players. Turn crossplay off and you can mix PS4 and PS5 players. There is also PS4 to PS5 autopop.
