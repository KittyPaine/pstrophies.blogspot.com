Quick Overview

For fans of the classic, pre-Siege Rainbow Six series, this game serves as their true spiritual successor. While Siege originally focused on hostage rescue before pivoting entirely to competitive bomb defusal, this title takes those tactical roots and delivers a pure PvE experience. Operating as a tactical unit, your objectives are to suppress and arrest terrorists, collect evidence, and rescue hostages. You have total tactical freedom to execute your missions using either lethal or non-lethal force.

  1. Walk up to a door safely and use your Mirrorgun to check for traps and locate enemies.
  2. Step back into a safe hallway corner.
  3. Open your command menu, select Red Team (or Both), and choose "Queue: C2, Gas, and Clear."
  4. Once they position themselves, hit the command to execute. The door will blow, the AI shield will walk in first, and the pepperball rifles will instantly suppress the room while you watch the hallway behind you.
  5. Look into the room and Use the Command "Search Room".
  6. If you are playing on a massive map and don't want to hunt for missed weapons or civilians, you can open the command menu and select "Trail Room" or "Secure Evidences" to force the AI to do a sweeping search.
  7. Aim at a door and Command "Use Wedge".

Justice Uncovers Depths Ghosts in Elysium

Equip C2, spawn in Loading Bay. Walk through the murky, waterlogged basement hallways. You are hunting for a specific structural concrete wall that has unique spray-painted graffiti symbols tagged onto it. It looks completely distinct from the rest of the ruined resort textures. Step up to the spray-painted section of the wall. The game will give you an prompt to place a C2 breach charge directly onto the concrete. Step back to a safe distance and blow the charge! The wall will completely collapse, revealing a hidden, dimly lit tactical bunker—the Secret MLO Meeting Room. Step into the hidden room. If a teammate does this in multiplayer the whole team gets credit.

Targeted Manipulation

To secure your safety, hunt down the signal jammer room on the third floor to block the primary bomb network frequency. Head to the top floor and neutralize the VIP leader ("Flower Head"). Do not execute or shoot him while he has his dead man's switch active. Instead, use non-lethal compliance (like a taser or pepper ball) to force him to surrender and kneel. When you handcuff/restrain him, he will drop his manual detonator onto the floor. Instead of bagging it as evidence, walk right up to it and hit the interaction prompt to manually detonate the explosives yourself!
