Quick Overview

The Sims 4 in a life simulation game part of The Sims series of games. It has a number of expansions but as there are no DLC trophies they are not required for the platinum. Unfortunately a few are RNG based so they may increase the time to platinum.

Difficulty Rating

3/10

Online/Offline Trophies

All trophies are possible offline.

Ultimate Sim Guru

The Platinum.

50-Mile-High-Club

To do this you will need a couple obviously, you also need a couple thousand simoleons. The Rocketship can be found under Outdoor activities. Having a Sim with high rocket science skill will help them to build the rocket faster. They will gain this naturally while working on the rocket but it can also be gained from skill books.

All Nighter

Pretty straight forward, have a Sim stay up for 24 Sim hours, to make this easier buy a coffee maker. Being buzzed will help them stay awake. Fast forwarding time helps but be careful they don't pass out.

Alphabet Legacy

This may seem a bit intimidating but there's an easy way to do it. Start a family with as many generations as possible. Then lock everyone but the newest generation in a room. Have the sim that's not locked up adopt a child and make them enough cakes to age them to a young adult. Then repeat this process. You'll know a Sim is close to death when they are Ravenous.

Are You Entertained?

Pretty much just have any Sim reach level 10 in the Entertainer career. To do this make sure your sim goes to work in a good mood has the required skills and does any required objectives to get promoted.

Basketcase

There are a number of ways of having Sims feel certain emotions but having emotion paintings can make this much easier.This guide lists all the emotions and how to trigger them.>

Beyond Repairs

Reading skill books will raise your Handiness skill, but repairing and upgrading objects also increases it.

Black Widow

Place a married couple in a household with 4 single male Sims. Kill off the husband by locking him in a room. Once he's dead, have her romance each remaining Sim, marry and kill them off.

Bow-dacious

Either read a skill book or practice playing the violin.

Captain of Industry

Cast Away

Either read a skill book or go fishing in a body of water.

Code Monk

To max out your programming skill it's best to own a computer and practice programming, this can also earn you some simoleons.

Connoisseurus Rex

You'll unlock this skill at cooking level 5, but there are only certain recipes that increase this skill so it's best to study a skill book.

Elementary

You can either use skill books, play chess or use a telescope. Working on a rocket ship also increases logic.

Fired Up

You can jog, use workout equipment at home or go to the gym.

Flying Colors

Pretty much just have any Sim reach level 10 in the Painter career. To do this make sure your sim goes to work in a good mood has the required skills and does any required objectives to get promoted.

Full House

Create a family of 8 Sims and place them in a house. A good idea to combine this with Ghostly/Ghost Family.

Games Within Games

Play lots of video games, you can do this on a tablet, computer or game console.

Ghost Family

Combine this with full house. Kill off 7 of the sims by locking them in a room. Then have the 8th sim try to befriend them as they appear, eventually You'll be able to invite them to move in. Once all the other ghosts have moved in kill off the final Sim then befriend their ghost to get them to move in.

Ghostly

Take care of all their needs except for social, in a Sim 24 period. Having social maxed before doing this is a good idea or doing this with a Sim who has the loner trait so they don't feel the negative effects.
