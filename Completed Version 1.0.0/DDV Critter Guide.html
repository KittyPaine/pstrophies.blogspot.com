Critters Schedule and Favourite Foods

Feeding a critter their favourite food 2-5 times, once per day, unlocks them as a companion.

Base Game

Plaza ~ Squirrels

Favourite Food is Peanuts, which can be bought from Remy's restaurant.

  • Black Squirrel ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • Classic Squirrel ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Grey Squirrel ~ Sun 12pm - 12am Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Red Squirrel ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day
  • White Squirrel ~ Sun 12am - 6am

Peaceful Meadow ~ Rabbits

You need to chase Rabbits a couple times to feed them. Rabbits Favourite Food is Carrots, seeds can be bought from Goofy in Peaceful Meadow.

  • Black Rabbit ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Brown Rabbit ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Calico Rabbit ~ Thurs 8am - 2pm
  • Classic Rabbit ~ Sat 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • White Rabbit ~ Sun 12am - 6am, Tues - Thurs All Day

Dazzle Beach ~ Sea Turtles

To feed Sea Turtles wait for their head to pop back out. Sea Turtles Favourite food is Seaweed. Seaweed can be caught in any body of water by fishing anywhere besides the ripples.

  • Black Sea Turtle ~ Mon 10am - 4pm
  • Brown Sea Turtle ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All day
  • Classic Sea Turtle ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Purple Sea Turtle ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • White Sea Turtle ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day

Forest of Valor ~ Raccoons

To feed Raccoons you need to approach when they are not standing on their hind legs. Runs a short distance then stop. Raccoons Favourite food is Blueberries, bushes can be found on Dazzle Beach and in Forest of Valor.

  • Black Raccoon ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Blue Raccoon ~ Wed 4pm - 10pm
  • Classic Raccoon ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Red Raccoon ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day
  • White Raccoon ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day

Glade of Trust ~ Crocodiles

To feed Crocodiles you need to approach when their head is down. Run a short distance then stop, same as the Raccoons. Crocodiles Favourite food is Lobster, they can be caught in orange ripples in the Glade of Trust.

  • Blue Crocodile ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day
  • Classic Crocodile ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Golden Crocodile ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • Pink Crocodile ~ Sat 6am - 12pm
  • Red Crocodile ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • White Crocodile ~ Sun 6pm - 12am

Sunlit Plateau ~ Sunbirds

Sunbirds each like certain colours of flowers. Emerald likes green or yellow. Golden likes orange or yellow. Orchid likes pink or purple. Red likes blue or red. Turquoise likes green or pink.

  • Emerald Sunbird ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Golden Sunbird ~ Sun 12am to 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • Orchid Sunbird ~ Fri 9am - 3pm
  • Red Sunbird ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Turquoise Sunbird ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day

Frosted Heights ~ Foxes

You need to chase Foxes a couple times to feed them. Foxes Favourite food is White Sturgeon, they can be caught from orange ripples in the Frosted Heights.

  • Black Fox ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Blue Fox ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • Classic Fox ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day
  • Red Fox ~ Sat 2am - 8am
  • White Fox ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day

Forgotten Lands ~ Ravens

Ravens Favourite food is any 5 star meal. The easiest one you can make is Large Seafood Platter.

  • Blue Raven ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Tues, Thurs, Fri All Day
  • Brown Raven ~ Tues 6pm - 12am
  • Classic Raven ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Mon, Thurs, Sat All Day
  • Red Raven ~ Sun 12am - 12pm, Mon, Wed, Fri All Day
  • White Raven ~ Sun 12pm - 12am, Tues, Wed, Sat All Day
Eternity Isle

Ancient's Landing ~ Monkeys

You need to chase Monkeys to feed them. Note: The monkey in The Ruins is glitchy and may reset while chasing it. Monkeys Favourite Food is the Banana Split.

  • Beige Monkey ~ The Ruins ~ Sun, Thurs All Day, Mon, Wed, Fri 12pm - 12am
  • Black and Brown Monkey ~ The Courtyard ~ Sun, Tues, Thurs 12pm - 12am, Wed, Sat All Day
  • Black and Grey Monkey ~ The Ruins ~ Tues 6pm - 12am
  • Classic Monkey ~ The Ruins ~ Sun - Sat All Day
  • Red and Beige Monkey ~ The Overlook ~ Mon, Wed, Fri 12am - 12pm, Tues, Sat, All Day
  • Toon Monkey ~ The Docks ~ Sun - Sat All Day

Glittering Dunes ~ Cobras

Cobras Favourite food is Eggs, which can be bought from Remy's restaurant. To feed Cobras you need to approach when they have their head down. Run a short distance then stop, same as the Raccoons and Crocodiles.

  • Blue and Red Striped Cobra ~ The Wastes ~ Sun, Thurs All Day, Mon, Tues, Sat 12pm - 12am
  • Classic Cobra ~ The Plains ~ Sun - Sat All Day
  • Green and White Striped Cobra ~ The Borderlands ~ Thurs 6am - 12pm
  • Pink Spotted Cobra ~ The Oasis ~ Sun, Wed, Thurs 12am - 12pm, Mon, Fri All Day
  • Yellow and Purple Striped Cobra ~ The Borderlands ~ Sun, Wed, Thurs 12pm to 12am, Tues, Sat All Day
  • Toon Cobra ~ The Wastes ~ Sun - Sat All Day

Wild Tangle ~ Capybaras

Capybaras Favourite food is Cabbage, which seeds can be bought from Goofy in The Grasslands.

  • Black and White Capybara ~ The Promenade ~ Sun, Fri, Sat 12pm - 12am, Tues, Thurs All Day
  • Blue Striped Capybara ~ The Grove ~ Mon - Wed 12am - 12pm, Thurs, Sat All Day

You need to chase Bees to feed them. Sweet Bees Favourite Food is Honey Coral, which can be caught in white ripples in Pixie Acres.

  • Blue Sweet Bee ~ Hunny Falls ~ Sun - Sat All Day (Clear Skies or Light Clouds)
  • Pink Sweet Bee ~ Pixie Flats ~ Wed, Fri and Sat All Day

Glamour Gulch ~ Skunk

To feed Skunks you need to approach when their tail is down. Run a short distance then stop, same as the Raccoons and Crocodiles. Skunks Favourite Foods are Button Mushroom, it can be found growing in Glamour Gulch and Pincushion Peach, trees can be found in Glamour Gulch.

  • Skunk ~ Runway River ~ Sun - Sat 12pm - 12am
  • Brown Skunk ~ Paisley Park ~ Sun, Mon, Wed, Fri 12pm - 12 am
  • White Skunk ~ Modish Marsh ~ Sun, Tue, Thurs, Sat 12pm - 12am
  • Haute Skunk ~ Haute Plateau ~ Sun - Sat All Day (Heavy Clouds, Heavy Rain, Light Rain or Stormy)
