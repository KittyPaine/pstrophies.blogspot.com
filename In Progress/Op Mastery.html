ATTACK ROLES
Hard Breach Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Hard breach operators are primarily responsible for site access. They open key walls to the site and create critical lines of sight, such as opening hatches. There are three main hard breachers, each with their own distinct strengths: Thermite, Ace, and Hibana.

  • Thermite: His core strength is creating maximum line-of-sight exposure and large walk-in openings.
  • Ace: Offers full immunity to Mute jammers and safe site opening from a distance, though he opens slightly smaller holes compared to Thermite.
  • Hibana: The undisputed queen of opening hatches. Thanks to the hatch health pool system, she uses significantly less utility to open hatches compared to Thermite or Ace.
Support Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Support is a broad term in Siege. It isn't limited to physical operator utility—like Doc's Stim Pistol or Valkyrie's Black Eye cameras. Support also means active yellow pings, drone work, and clear vocal callouts.

Because of this, even an eliminated player can actively support their team and lead them to victory. The scoreboard doesn't dictate your true KOST or your impact on the win rate.

Solo Queue Reality Check: You don't need to top-frag to carry rounds. Providing precise, actionable intel—like yellow-pinging defenders or calling out swings for your entry players—can single-handedly maintain a high win rate.
Intel / Counter-Intel Core Objective

Intel can make or break a round. Knowing precise camera placement is essential—you need high-visibility angles that aren't easily spotted or destroyed. In higher-tier play, Yellow Pings and voice callouts are almost always superior to Red Pings, as a Red Ping instantly alerts the enemy that they are being watched, prompting them to destroy the camera or alter their position.

  • Strategic Placement: Hide cameras in ambient environment debris, dark ceiling corners, or behind destroyed props. Long-term surveillance outweighs temporary 360-degree vision.
  • Yellow Ping / Voice Callout Protocol: Use non-invasive callouts to keep enemies oblivious right up until a teammate swings or lines up a wallbang.
  • When to Red Ping: Reserve active scans for chaotic last-second site executes, syncing precise wallbangs, or pressuring a roamer alongside a Jackal or Deimos track.
  • Default Camera Clearance: Memorizing default camera locations on attack is vital. Stripping basic defender vision in the first 10 seconds strips away their early round awareness and opens safe entry routes.
Intel Tip: Don't give away your team's tactical advantage for a free scan score. Maintain camera stealth until a scan directly guarantees a kill or ensures a defuser plant.
Fragger Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Fragging isn't for everyone, so don't feel bad if pure mechanical gunskill isn't your main strength. Siege is built on intel and teamwork first. However, if you have reliable callouts or a yellow ping from a teammate, line up your crosshair, trust the swing, and commit to the duel with confidence.

  • Intel-Backed Swings: Never swing completely blind. Use live drone intel, yellow pings, and audio cues to pre-aim common defender power positions before breaking line of sight.
  • Aim & Recoil Practice: Spend dedicated time in the Shooting Range to fine-tune your target acquisition speed and adjust your look sensitivity until recoil control feels second nature.
  • Map Flow & Prefiring: Practice map runs and target drills (like Clear House) to build muscle memory for crosshair placement at head height and pre-firing common anchor angles.
Aim Tip: Confidence is half the battle. Use warm-up drills in the Shooting Range to dial in your sensitivity so you can swing on fresh intel without second-guessing your recoil control.
DEFENSE ROLES
Roamer Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Most players misinterpret roaming as purely hunting for kills, but a roamer's true primary directive is time waste. Every second an attacker spends chasing you through off-site hallways or clearing rooms is time stolen from their final site push and defuser plant execution.

  • Delay Over Frags: Forcing attackers to burn drone utility and waste clock management is a massive win, even if you don't land a single kill. Prioritize survival over greedy swings.
  • Dual-Role Flexibility: Certain operators naturally blend roaming with secondary utility—such as Caveira acting as a Roamer/Intel hybrid, using Silent Step to manipulate map presence and Silent Interrogations to expose the entire enemy team.
  • Anchor Pincer Synergy: Coordinate directly with your site anchors. When an attacker focuses their push on an anchor holding a key site angle, execute a late-round flank to strike while their attention is completely split.
Roamer Tip: Your value is measured by the timer, not just the kill feed. Stalling an attack until the 30-second mark forces a desperate, uncoordinated site rush that your anchors can clean up with ease.
Trapper Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Trappers function as crucial utility sinks and time wasters. Their primary goal is to disrupt attacker momentum, slow down aggressive site rushes, and force enemies to waste valuable explosive utility or time dealing with traps before they can enter critical choke points.

  • Shield & Rush Denial: Traps severely punish shield operators and fast-paced pushes by inflicting damage, applying visual/audio disorientation, or physically restricting movement.
  • Key Trapper Roster: Core trappers and entry-denial operators include Kapkan, Jäger, Frost, Lesion, Ela, Wamai, Melusi, Aruni, Thorn, Fenrir, and Denari.
  • Intel & Sound Cues: Beyond pure damage, triggered traps act as immediate audio proxies, alerting your anchors and roamers to exact attacker push directions.
Trap Synergy (Lethal Combinations): Layer your traps for guaranteed kills! Pairing Thorn + Lesion forces attackers into a nightmare scenario where the Gu Mine prevents sprinting out of the Razorbloom blast zone while inflicting tick damage. Alternatively, pairing Thorn + Frost ensures a guaranteed finish when a trapped attacker can't escape the detonation in time.
Intel / Counter-Intel Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

On defense, vision control dictates round tempo. Camera placement is everything—you need lines of sight that stay alive as long as possible while remaining vigilant against destruction or gadget theft from Brava's Kludge Drones. Furthermore, sound cues are just as critical as visual feed for tracking enemy pinpoints through walls.

  • Intel Gathering & Counter-Intel: Deployable cameras, bulletproof cams, and static site cams map out attacker pushes. Operators like Mozzie actively deny attacker scouting while stealing their drones to turn their own vision network against them.
  • Audio Surveillance: Don't rely solely on visual feeds. Listening for footsteps, breach deployments, and utility audio cues gives precise directional awareness without exposing camera positions.
  • Anti-Hacking Precautions: Place high-value intel devices in angles that force Brava to expose her Kludge Drone to destroyers, preventing attackers from hijacking your own defense network.
Intel Tip: Sound cues don't give off a red ping warning. Use your headset alongside hidden cams to track attacker movements silently, giving your anchors or roamers the perfect timing for a surprise swing.
Anchor Core Objective

Key Responsibilities & Detailed Breakdown:

Anchors are the backbone of site defense. Holding site control is vital, which is why a solid setup requires at least 2–3 anchors on site at all times. If an anchor falls, roamers must recognize the collapse and immediately rotate back to site to reinforce power positions and prevent a breach execution.

  • 3-Armor Profile: Most dedicated anchors are heavy 3-Armor operators. Since their heavy movement makes the most noise, remaining stationary on site and holding tight lines of sight maximizes their health pool without giving away position through footstep audio.
  • Angle Discipline & Repositioning: Holding tight angles is essential for shutting down entry points. However, if an attacker spots or pings your location, reposition immediately—holding a burnt angle leaves you completely vulnerable to a swift pre-fire.
  • Late-Round Plant Denial: Anchors absorb initial attacker utility push and use site geometry, shields, and area-denial gadgets to stall defuser plants as the clock runs down.
Anchor Tip: Don't fall in love with a single sightline. The moment an attacker drones you out or fires a test spray at your cover, shift to a secondary angle on site before they swing on fresh intel.

⚔️ Attackers

Sledge 3 Armor / 1 Speed
How to Master This Operator's Key Role: Sledge is often misplayed as a simple entry fragger, but his true value lies in executing high-value utility denial and vertical pressure without consuming limited attacker explosives. The Tactical Breaching Hammer (Caber) instantly destroys Barbed Wire, Castle Panels, Kiba Barriers, and Deployable Shields with a single swing—bypassing electro-charged surfaces or signal disruptors entirely. When taking top-down control above the objective, prioritize line-of-sight creation over raw gunfights by stripping away soft flooring to expose metal beams. This allows you to drop utility between beams, flush anchored defenders out of safe positions, and systematically dismantle site setups while preserving your team's secondary gadgets.
