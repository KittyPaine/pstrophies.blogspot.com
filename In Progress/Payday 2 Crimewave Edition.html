Quick Overview

Welcome to my walkthrough for Payday 2: Crimewave Edition. I will explain each trophies requirements, the difficulty trophies will be a bit different because they now stack. I will provide stealth methods and videos for easily stealthable heists. DLC Trophies are included at the bottom, they are in order of the PSN listing.

Online/Offline Trophies

Approximately 90% of the trophy list can be completed solo using AI teammates. However, several trophies require four human players due to their coordination requirements. While all difficulty trophies are easier with a team, the improved Crimewave Edition AI makes solo completion a viable option.

Build Notes

This is my general-purpose stealth build used throughout PAYDAY 2. The core skill setup remains unchanged, as it provides access to drill improvements, civilian control, stealth utility, and objective flexibility. Depending on the heist, I recommend swapping equipment and weapons as needed rather than changing the underlying skill tree.

  • ECMs: Recommended for ECM-focused stealth strategies and objective rushing.
  • Trip Mines: Recommended when additional guard tracking and map awareness are required.
  • Shaped Charges: Recommended for heists that benefit from breaching shortcuts or completing objectives more efficiently.
  • OVE9000 Saw: Recommended for deposit boxes, ATMs, and other sawable objectives.

The build uses the Plainsrider Bow from The Butcher's Western Pack by default. Players who do not own the DLC can substitute the recommended CAR-4 configuration listed below without requiring any changes to the skill tree.

  • Short Barrel
  • Stubby Compensator
  • Concealment Boost
  • E.M.O. Foregrip
  • Rubber Grip
  • Vintage Magazine
  • Folding Stock

Note: Akimbo Broomstick Pistols are missing from the build site but they share the exact same attachments as the secondary Broomstick Pistol so you can use it as a reference.

This setup provides a fully viable base-game alternative while maintaining the concealment required for stealth gameplay.

These builds are based on the loadouts I personally used throughout my time with PAYDAY 2. My primary loud build was originally a Crit-Dodge Rogue setup centered around Akimbo Broomstick Pistols, which became my preferred loadout after extensive experimentation with weapons, perk decks, armor configurations, and playstyles. The original build was later featured by a member of the PAYDAY 2 community, who showcased it in a gameplay video and credited me by my PSN ID.

While revisiting my old loadouts for this guide, I found that my approach to build design had evolved considerably. Rather than simply recreating the original setups, I re-evaluated them using a prevention-focused mindset aimed at reducing overall failure rates. Skills, equipment, and weapon modifications were reassessed based on their ability to prevent common causes of failure, such as special enemies, poor positioning, or prolonged exposure, rather than their ability to recover from mistakes after they occurred. As a result, the builds presented here are refined versions of the originals, incorporating both my past experience and insights gained from years of further analysis and guide creation.

Important: The builds assume the Infamy skill cost reduction that lowers the point requirement needed to unlock the top tier of the tree. If you have not reached Infamy, you may not have enough points to complete the build exactly as shown. In that case, I recommend removing Silent Killer from the stealth build, as it provides the least overall utility compared to the build's core stealth, drill, saw, and crowd-control skills.

< 5812 = bar below or equal to 401
> 5783 = bar above or equal to 399

Don't Bring the Heat

Pre-Planning

  • Place a Body Bag Case in the West Stairwell.
  • Place a Keycard in the Management Corridor.
  • Vote for the Elevator Trick escape.

Once the heist begins, the host should handle the active stealthing as desync is less severe for them. If playing with a partner, they can stay in casing mode to scout and mark guards for you.

1. Locate Keycards & Key Box

Your first step in casing mode is finding a keycard, which spawns in a random location:

  • On the counters behind the tellers
  • On a desk in the upstairs or downstairs offices
  • On the coffee table just outside the upstairs offices
  • On the counter or tables in the cafeteria
  • On the desk inside one of the manager's offices

Optionally, keep an eye out for the key box (a white box marked GenSec). It will be on the wall by the door leading to the offices behind the tellers, in one of the manager's offices, or on a wall inside the vault area.

2. Clear the Front Guards & Roaming Civilians

Locate the server door, which spawns either downstairs across from the cafeteria, beside the West Stairwell, or in the management corridor. Check for cameras in the stairwell or corridor. If present, mask up and break them one at a time to lure the 2 front guards to your location. Eliminate them out of sightlines, answer their pagers, and bag their bodies if necessary.

There are 4 wandering managers: a woman in a red pantsuit, a woman in a blue skirt, an old lady, and a male manager (who has a 50% chance of carrying a keycard). Cable tie them whenever an opportunity opens up.

3. Set the Fake Drill Decoy

Once both front guards are dealt with, knock the painting off the safe in the manager's office and place an un-upgraded basic drill on it. Once the drill jams, leave it broken-it acts as a decoy to lock roaming guard pathing in place. Standing near the broken drill lets you see the outlines of guards through the wall; if they stop moving, they are trapped by your decoy.

4. Access Server & Hack Terminals

Open the server room door using an ECM Jammer—DO NOT use your keycard here. You need to save both keycards to disable the laser grid on the vault doors later.

Start the server hack. The host (or solo player) must locate the correct computer desk:

  • Co-op: The host interacts with the server terminal while the client checks the office computers until Bain highlights the correct one.
  • Solo: You must check each office computer one by one. You can interact with them through divider walls if you can see the yellow outline.

5. Timelock, Phone Call & Elevator Event

Once you get the code, activate the timelock. Position the host in the manager's office by the phone watching the upper elevator, while your partner watches the 1st floor elevator. When Bain calls, answer immediately. Note: Killing civilians increases the chance of Bain failing the phone response!

If the call triggers extra security, Bain will announce elevators moving to your floor. You will get either 2 civilians or a civilian and a guard. Deal with them immediately; if out of cable ties, eliminate them. There are 5 guards total in the back area (4 roamers and 1 camera operator). Ideally, the 4 roamers will get stuck in the stairwell near your decoy drill.

6. Open the Vault

Locate the camera room—it spawns at the top of the right stairwell, downstairs by the reception counter, or upstairs by reception. Eliminate the camera operator if necessary.

  1. Make your way to the fingerprint scanner and activate it.
  2. Press the 2 buttons on opposite sides of the room. Take your time and play patient.
  3. Have one player return to the manager's office computer to retrieve the final vault code.
  4. Insert the code, open the vault, and use your saved keycards to unlock the laser cages inside.

7. Secure the Loot

Bag all the money and stack it near the vault door. Optionally, pick the money counter safe if you have Nimble Aced. Move through the center room carefully, watching for active cameras or remaining guards.

Open the elevator doors in the pre-planned escape sector and drop all loot bags directly into the open escape hatch. Once every bag is secured, enter the hatch to complete the heist!

Blind Eye in the Sky

Pre-Planning

  • Have the back doors unlocked.
  • Purchase Heartbreaker Annie.
  • Add the keys to your inventory.
  • Select the Limo Escape.

There are 3 briefcases to locate during the heist. One can spawn behind the VIP Bar, one in the Locker Room, and one inside the safe located in the Manager's Office. You can safely eliminate the 3 guards roaming the back area. Save the 4th pager for emergencies.

1. Acquire the Guest List

Retrieve the blueprints from the Archives and fax them to Bain. After a short wait, Bain will provide a room number. Enter the indicated room, collect the USB drive, and obtain the guest list.

2. Poison the VIP

Locate the coloured bottle that matches the one displayed on the VIP's slot machine and spike his drink.

  • VIP Bar
  • Tiki Bar by the Pool
  • Upstairs (2 possible spawns)

Having a teammate remain in civilian mode to scout bottle locations can make this section much easier.

Once the VIP drinks the cocktail, he will head to the bathroom to vomit. Follow him and steal his keycard.

3. Access Security

Use the stolen keycard to enter the room Bain identified. Place the sleeping gas into the ventilation system and wait for it to take effect.

Once the area is secure, enter the Security Office and deactivate the laser system.

4. Open the Vault

Proceed to the vault and wait for Bain to provide the code sequence. Input the numbers he gives you to unlock the vault.

The Dentist's loot is hidden behind a locked door containing a laser puzzle. Carefully navigate the lasers and secure the additional loot.

5. Secure the Loot & Escape

Place all loot bags in the centre of the elevator platform. Avoid placing bags near the edges, as they can fall off during transport.

Once all loot has been secured, use the limo escape to complete the heist.
