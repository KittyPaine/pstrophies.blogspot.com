Quick Overview

After a tragic incident that claims the life of Diane Fisher and leaves Sal disfigured, he and his father relocate to the Addison Apartments. As Sal adjusts to his new life, he begins to uncover the dark history of the building and its residents. What starts as an attempt to make new friends quickly spirals into a hunt for the truth. Things aren't what they seem—involving murder, malevolent spirits, and a mysterious cult pulling the strings from the shadows.
