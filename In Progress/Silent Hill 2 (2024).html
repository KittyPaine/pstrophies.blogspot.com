Quick Overview

This is the Remake of the 2001 classic psychological horror. It does the franchise justice other than a few unneeded changes it's a fairly well done Remake. It even retains the separate difficulty settings for combat and puzzles, with new puzzles having been added to spice things up. It's got an easy platinum as everything can be done on the lowest difficultly settings.

No Turning Back Now

After gaining control turn around and head left down the highway.

Enjoy Your Stay

Proceed down the stairs from the Observation Deck, head through the graveyard where you meet Angela and enter Silent Hill.

Nice And Cozy

Collect the Neely's Bar coin from the cash register in Texan Café. While exploring the south end of Martin St, you can find a matchbook on a corpse that marks Neely's Bar on your map. Inside the bar, inspect the broken jukebox to get half a record, alongside a note pointing toward Groovy Music and Saul Street Apartments. Proceed north to Groovy Music, smash the interior glass to find the other half of the record and vinyl glue, then combine them in your menu. Head to the Saul Street Apartments next to the auto parts shop. Grab the Apartment 5 key from the front desk office, head to the second floor, and navigate through Apt 5's cracked walls to reach the outside fire escape. Climb up to enter Apartment 9 on the third floor. Go to the bathroom and interact with the hole in the wall 3 times to pull out the missing Jukebox Button. Return to Neely's Bar, load the record, place the button, insert the coin, and press C2. Take the Neely's Bar Key from the machine and use it to unlock the bar's back door. Follow the linear path through the back alleys until a cutscene plays; you will find the Woodside Apartments Key inside a pile of bloody clothes on the street near a blue car shortly after.

Let's NOT Party!

You'll find the handgun in Apt. 217 in a shopping cart. The Balloons are located in Apt. 207. I suggest you save before shooting the balloons to conserve your ammo.

Otherworldly

You enter the Otherworld for the first time after fighting off an old friend (you don't actually have to attack him, the fight is timed.) Upon exiting through the red door you will be in Blue Creek apartments, an Otherworldly version of Woodside Apartments.

Passed

You get this after entering the door marked S aka Scott or Seconds.

Uncanny

Reach Rosewater Park and walk down the boardwalk to the Gazebo.

All Seems In Order

When you first arrive at Jack's Inn after meeting Maria you, enter room 106 and James says that they should leave as something is 'wrong' with it. You must wait to return here after visiting Brookhaven hospital. Upon your return examine the damaged wall which wasn't there before. The symbol is the Halo of Sun. A reference to Silent Hill 3, Heather and Douglas stays at Jack's Inn when they arrive in Silent Hill.

Leftovers

After exiting Rosewater park, pay a visit to Pete's Bowl-o-Rama to find leftover pizza from the original game from when you find Laura and Eddie.

Admitted

After opening the gate and passing through Heaven's Night you see Laura run into Brookhaven Hospital, follow her inside.

Nightmare Fuel

After finding Laura she says she forgot something, a letter from Mary. But it's a trick, instead she locks you in a hospital room so make sure you are ready for a fight.

Alone Again

You find Maria in the basement after exploring for a bit. Once she's with you, you solve puzzles and collect the rings together. The chase sequence triggers afterward; you sprint to the elevator. Pyramid Head catches Maria at the elevator doors, and she is "killed".

Into The Abyss

After retrieving the key from Rosewater Park head to the Silent Hill Historical Society. Inside you will find a seemingly endless staircase.

Unforgivable

In the underground Labyrinth you will eventually find Angela in a room with pistons in the walls and she is being attacked by a monster that looks like 2 figures, one on top of the other, in a bed. Save her. (Mature themes. Viewer Discretion is advised.)

Inner Sanctum

This refers to Pyramids Head's room. It is a circular room and in it you will find the Great Knife which he has discarded in favour of a spear. It is slow and heavy but does a great deal of damage. Originally there were supposed to be 2, indicating it was actually a pair of giant scissors.

A Human Being

After making it through Toluca Prison you'll find Eddie but he's acting strange. He's loses his mind and attacks you with a revolver be you are "laughing at him", defend yourself. This fight can be rather difficult, even on normal, due to the fog. Dodging is essential for this fight. Dodge his shots and strike when you spot him.

Glimmer of Hope

After defeating Eddie you exit onto a dock with a rowboat waiting for you on Toluca Lake, make your way across. She is waiting for you.

Scourge Of Toluca Lake

If you are doing the speed run, which I do recommend leaving for your second run, save before leaving the dock and you can afk until this pops.

It's Bread

This is another Silent Hill 3 reference, in the bakery in the mall Heather comments on bread that she finds on the counter. If you interact with it she says, "It's bread." It has become a popular meme in the fan community.
