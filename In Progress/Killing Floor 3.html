Quick Overview

Killing Floor 3 is the third Entry in the Killing Floor series, a co-op horde shooter. But instead of zombies it has clones called Zeds. 3 is a noticeable improvement over 2 gameplay wise. And no, there are no collectable trophies this time around.

Difficulty Rating

Singleplayer 5/10 Multiplayer 9/10 Due to difficulty scaling.

Online/Offline Trophies

Online only and crossplay with PC is supported.
