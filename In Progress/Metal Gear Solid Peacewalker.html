Main OPs 33 Unlock Conditions

Completing the Zadornov search sequence is required to unlock the later Main Ops. Make sure ZEKE is assembled and docked at Mother Base, and that your Intel Team and Mission Support Rank meet the requirements.

  1. Zadornov Searches 1-6: Complete all six official Zadornov Search missions in Main Ops.
  2. ZEKE Basic Assembly: Collect and attach the Head, Legs, Right Arm, and Left Arm parts.
  3. ZEKE Railgun (Mandatory): Attach the Railgun obtained from Chrysalis. This is required to trigger Main Op 33 and for related boss fight trophies.
  4. Mother Base Assignment: Remove ZEKE from any active Outer Ops deployments so it is docked at base.
  5. Intel Unit Level: Raise your Intel Team to approximately level 40 or higher to trigger the final Mother Base Command Post expansion.

    Pro tip — Fast-track Intel level 40: Farm high-stat staff in these missions:

    • Main Op: Infiltrate the Crater Base (free S-Rank): Inspect the truck with license plate 63824 during the mission to extract Hideo Kojima, who has an S-Rank Intel stat.
    • Main Op: Destroy the Barricade (2 S-Rank Intel recruits): At Camino de Lava: Junction, look toward the high sniper cliff. Both snipers have S-Rank Intel stats. Extract them using the Fulton Launcher or a Fulton Mine placed against the wall below their ledge.
    • Extra Ops 014 / 016 (A- or B-Rank ground route): If you don't have Fulton Mines or the Fulton Launcher, run either mission with the Mk. 22 and Sneaking Suit. Extract the ground guards for A- or B-Rank Intel staff.
  6. Mother Base Development: Your Mission Support Rank, visible in Mother Base data, must be at least 2. Once the Command Center reaches Rank 6, complete missions to raise its rank progress.
  7. Trigger Kaz's Call: Play 1-3 brief Extra Ops missions until Miller calls to report Zadornov's seventh escape.
  8. Locate Zadornov (Extra Op 001): Sortie as Snake into Extra Op 001: Target Practice. Head to the second-floor catwalk and locate Zadornov behind the wall.

Prerequisites & Endgame Setup: MSF Level 99 & Essential S-Rank Gear

To clear every mission with an S-Rank on a solo run, you will need high-level non-lethal equipment. Weapons like the Carl Gustav (Fulton Recovery Launcher) require high R&D and team levels—often near or at Level 99. Use the references below to farm S-Rank staff, collect key non-lethal blueprints, and improve your gear ranks before tackling harder S-rank times. To fast-track research, repeat Extra Op 010.

1. S-Rank Staff Farming Routes (Level 99 Teams)

Note: Always bring a Rank 3+ Analyzer (to read text skills), the Sneaking Suit, and Fulton Mines or the Carl Gustav (FR) to extract targets on elevated cliffs.

  • Combat Team: Extra Ops 083 (Custom Armored Vehicle) or Extra Ops 079-082 — Escort soldiers accompanying high-level vehicle battles frequently spawn with S-Rank Combat stats. Drop smoke grenades in their patrol paths and detonate Fulton Mines to extract groups simultaneously.
  • R&D Team: Extra Ops 049 (Perfect Stealth) — Complete the mission to extract the 3 POWs. They have a high baseline chance to roll with S-Rank R&D stats alongside rare end-game technology skills.
  • Intel Team: Main Op — Destroy the Barricade — Advance to the Camino de Lava: Junction zone. Look at the high cliff ledges on the far side; the two snipers stationed there are S-Rank Intel units. Tranquilize them, then extract them using the Carl Gustav (FR) or by detonating a Fulton Mine placed at the base of the wall.
  • Medical Team: Extra Ops 026 (Eliminate Enemy Soldiers) or Extra Ops 050 — In Extra Op 026, a guaranteed S-Rank Medical POW is lying near the stairs at the end of the technical corridor. Alternatively, the bridge guard patrolling the ravine in Extra Op 050 is a guaranteed S-Rank Medical recruit.
  • Mess Hall Team: Extra Ops 046 (Perfect Stealth) — High Mess Hall stats raise overall Mother Base morale. In the starting area (Camino de Lava: Hillside), the second patrolling guard consistently rolls with S-Rank Cooking stats. Hold him up or tranquilize him for a clean extraction.

2. Blueprint Locations

  • Carl Gustav (Fulton Recovery Launcher): Complete Extra Op 098. It fires remote Fulton warheads and is useful for extracting distant soldiers and vehicle escorts from cover.
  • Infinity Bandana: Earn an S-Rank on Extra Op 119 (Peace Walker Custom). Grants unlimited ammunition; requires R&D Lv. 88 and Combat Lv. 88 to develop.
  • M47: Earn an S-Rank on Extra Op 111 (Attack Chopper Battle: AH56A-Raider Custom). This is the strongest lethal tracking rocket launcher in the game and is useful for damaging AI Mechs quickly.

3. How Gear Ranks & Level 5 Development Work

  1. Team Level Thresholds: Unlocking Rank 5 developments requires high baseline team levels, typically with R&D, Intel, and Medical levels between 60 and 90+.
  2. Linear Upgrades: Develop Ranks 1 through 4 of a weapon before its Rank 5 variant becomes available in the R&D menu.
  3. Advancing Time: Development requires sufficient GMP. Once research begins, complete 3 to 5 quick Extra Ops (such as Target Practice) to advance the in-game clock and finalize development.

4. Special Staff Skills Progression Gates

To check your roster for these skills at Mother Base, open Staff Management and toggle the display style to “Sort by Skill” to move them to the top of your list.

  1. Patriot: Extra Op 049 — Randomly rolled upon extracting any of the 3 main POWs on the map. Required for the infinite-ammo Patriot rifle.
  2. Japanese Patriot / Yamato Spirits: Extra Op 049 — Dropped by the Intel-focused female POW or the POW resting on top of the table. Required to develop the tornado-summoning Tanegashima Musket.
  3. Metamaterials Technology: Extra Op 049 — Found on the 3 main POWs. Required to research Stealth Camouflage.
  4. Electromagnetic Development: Extra Op 049 — A rare tech skill tied to the stage's 3 POWs. Required for the Rail Gun.
  5. Anti-Tank Rifle Development: Extra Op 055 — Look at the high industrial crane structures and Fulton the enemy sniper stationed there. Required for the PTRD-19 and PTRS-41.
  6. Optical Technology: Extra Op 049 — A rare tech trait found by repeatedly rescuing the stage's POWs. Required for Rank 5 laser-sighted firearms and Chaff Grenades.
  7. M134 Design: Extra Op 048 (Perfect Stealth) — Proceed to the second map zone; one of the active patrolling guards has this skill. Required for the M134 Gatling Gun.

Pass 1 (Progression & Recon): Use the sector enemy counts and guard locations to clear your path safely, making sure to extract all marked POWs along the way. These POWs are essential for recruiting high-rank personnel and unlocking skills that help level up Mother Base. This initial run lets you learn the sector layout, test guard lines of sight, and identify key trigger spots without worrying about the timer.

Pass 2 (S-Rank Execution): Once your Mother Base level is solid and you are ready for the S-Rank, re-execute the same stealth and CQC routes, but skip POW extractions. Bypassing the extractions saves time while letting you rely on the guard paths you learned during your first pass.

Highly recommend co-op for the vehicle and AI battles to make things easier, especially if your co-op partner has late-game gear.
