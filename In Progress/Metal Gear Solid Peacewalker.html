Quick Overview

Set in 1974 Costa Rica, Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker follows Big Boss as he builds his private military company, Militaires Sans Frontières, while uncovering a covert nuclear plot. Built around a mission-based loop, gameplay pairs tactical stealth and combat operations with offshore Mother Base management, where you Fulton-extract enemy soldiers to staff R&D, Intel, and Medical departments to craft higher-tier weapons and gear. Designed for both solo play and co-op, the game features extensive Main and Extra Ops, including a famous *Monster Hunter* crossover on *Isla del Monstruo* where players team up with modern military firepower to hunt legendary beasts like Rathalos, Tigrex, and Gear Rex for exclusive gear.
