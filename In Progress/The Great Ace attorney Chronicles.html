Quick Overview

The Great Ace Attorney Chronicles is a collection containing The Great Ace Attorney: Adventures and The Great Ace Attorney 2: Resolve. Set in the late 19th century, the games follow Ryunosuke Naruhodo, an aspiring defense attorney and ancestor of Phoenix Wright, as he travels from Japan to Great Britain and begins his legal career. Alongside his friends and allies, Ryunosuke becomes involved in a series of interconnected mysteries that span both nations while uncovering secrets connected to the British legal system.
