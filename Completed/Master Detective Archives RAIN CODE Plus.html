Quick Overview

From the creative minds behind Danganronpa comes an all-new mystery experience. Trade the high school corridors for the rain-slicked, neon-drenched streets of Kanai Ward. Partnered with a mischevious Shinigami, you aren't just uncovering a killer—you're taking on the all-powerful Amaterasu Corporation in a battle to solve the city's 'Ultimate Secret'.

First Day on the Job

Requests are show as a green ? icon on the map.

If you miss even one request, you will have to replay that chapter and complete all requests in that chapter playthrough even if you have completed them previously, as that playthrough overwrites that particular chapter save and the previous progress is erased.

Chapter 1

  • Detective Work? - Given by Vivia when you go to find the other detevtives at the Hotel, he is under the piano.
  • Paired with the Devil - A young woman in found outside the Mansion (2nd crime scene) in Ginma District. You can get this requesr after Halara joins you to Investigate the previous Nail Man killings.
  • Snatch the Snatcher - A young woman found in Kamasaki district has had her purse snatched.
  • Spitited Away - A young couple daughter found in Ginma district, their daughter has gone missing the Kamasaki distrit.

Chapter 2

Make sure to complete the requests before heading to the Academy with Desuhiko, otherwise you will be comletely locked out of the doing them and will have to do them in chapter select.
  • Kanai Ward's Underbelly - A yound man found right outside the detective agency along along the riverbank.
  • A Gift for the Girl - After finding Desuhiko in Ginma district, head to Kanai Station and speak to a suspicious looking man.
  • Lending the Church a Hand - Speak to the Nun at the Church.
  • Save the Servant - Find the Servant from the Church in a alley in Kamasaki district.

Chapter 3

  • A Teacher's Distress - Return to the Acadamy and speak to the teacher that Desuhiko was inpersonating.
  • Rumors of Red Rain - After gaining access to Marunomon district and placing the first camera speak to the frightened man on the bridge.
  • Young Hearts Want to Break Free - Speak to the Youngster in front of the Clocktower in Ginma District.
  • Troubles of the Heart - Speak to the Student outside the Cafe in Ginma district. It isthe Cafe across from the Cafe where you find Fubuki.

Chapter 4

  • Her name is Fukubi, the Legendary Waitress! - You find him at the Cafe in Ginma district where you found Fubuki is CH 3.
  • Lending the Church a Hand Again - Speak to the Nun at the church again.
  • Lost in the Rain - Speak to Iruka by the Clocktower in Ginma district.
  • The Fortune Teller Sees All - Return to the flooded Marunomon and speak to the Suspicious-Sounding Man then look for the Fortune Teller.
