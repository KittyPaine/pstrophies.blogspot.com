Quick Overview

The Blackout Club is about some small town kids who start a club to investigate the strange activities of the adults at night. They appear to move about as if in a trance, what could be causing this? And those strange symbols, is there a cult controlling the town?

Difficulty Rating

5/10

Online/Offline Trophies

It can be played online with up to 3 other players but can be played solo. However some trophies do require other players.

Showing all Trophies

The Disappearance

Basically complete the Tutorial.

Look at Me

This requires another player. You can join a random session and use any gesture. If you need to boost you can join the official Discord or try PSNP.

Not a Doctor

Either find a bandage in a supply box or choose the minor perk that let's you start with one.

My Hero

A player becomes suppressed when they are captured by the Shape. Obviously requires another player. If you need to boost you can join the official Discord or try PSNP.

Now You've Done It

When the Host is at least level 6, the Shape will change to Angel mode. All players will get this trophy.

... But Not Forgotten

Being captured by the Shape 3 times kills you. In order to do this you must be revived by another player.

Fashion Plate

After a mission go to the wardrobe and change your appear with any of the default options or buy something with the snacks you've earned.

Beat It, Kid!

A Stalker is a human player who had invaded your game. This requires 3 players and the host to be level 10. If you need to boost you can join the official Discord or try PSNP.

Believer

You must sacrifice a Light of Rebellion which is a special item you can receive in a large reward crate after completing a mission. Collect Bonus Evidence to boost your chances of getting a crate. It reportedly drops more often if you are below level 7.

True Power

The soonest you can get is by reaching level 10 and maxing out Takedown.

Monkeywrench

As the Stalker collect 500 points worth of evidence against The Blackout Club. To become the Stalker go to door with the eye symbol located in your hideout. You need 250 snacks to purchase a Stalker dossier. This requires 3 players and the host to be level 10. If you need to boost you can join the official Discordor try PSNP.

Winning Streak

Get 3 Wins in a row.

Ashes to Ashes

Go to the altar in your hideout and sacrifice your character at level 20.
