I Should Start a Circus Chapter 1
  • The first one is mandatory. You will find it by looking in the top right of Hajime's cottage and seeing it plainly against the wall.
  • Rocketpunch Market. Move the camera so you can see to the right of the vending machine, and the Hidden Monokuma is right next to it.
  • Airport. Look to the right, and you should see it riding one of the bags on the baggage claim conveyor belt.
  • Inside the Old Building's Office. Turn left to look at the shelves, and it's hiding in the bottom.
  • Inside Byakuya's cottage. Look to the top left and you should find it peeking outside the window.
Chapter 2
  • Library. Look up and to the right, and you'll see the Hidden Monokuma on the second floor.
  • Drug Store. This one might be a little hard to spot. It's hiding behind shelf on the far right side of the screen. Move the camera to the left ever so slightly and keep looking in the top right, and you should be able to see him.
  • Chandler Beach. Go all the way out to the left side of the ocean, and you'll see it floating there. Beach House Closet. Look at the top left and you'll see it peeking over the wall.
  • Inside Mahiru's cottage. It's halfway behind the TV, very easy to spot.
Chapter 3
  • Electric Ave. Look to the left and you'll find a huge Monokuma looking out a window above Kazuichi.
  • Theater. Just look to the right and you'll see him on the wall behind the velvet rope.
  • Hospital. Walk all the way to the end of the hall and look through the glass to your right, and he's floating like a ghost.
  • Hospital. Go upstairs into the Conference Room, you'll see him up above the lights, but it's dark up there, so he might blend in a little.
  • Motel. Inside Hiyoko's room, turn the camera so it's on the far right of the room, and you'll see the Monokuma in the bathroom.
Chapter 4
  • Nezumi Castle. Right when you enter, you should see it on the top left peeking above the wall.
  • Rollercoaster. Look as far right as you can and you'll see him in one of the cars.
  • Strawberry House 3F. Right when you leave the Park, look to your left and you'll find the Monokuma in a pail.
  • Monokuma Archive. Investigate the statue to the left of the door.
  • Strawberry House 2F Standard Room. You should see him behind the desk.
Chapter 5
  • Vendor Street. The Monokuma is on the right eating Ramen.
  • Factory. Look to the right at the basket of Monokuma plushies, and you'll see one standing on the top. That's the Hidden Monokuma.
  • Military Base. Look to the left, and you'll see him peeking out from the top of the red containers.
  • Sea King Industries. Look to the right, and covered up in some darkness and behind the railings is the Monokuma.
  • Inside Nagito's cottage. Look to the right and you'll find it in the bathroom.
Chapter 6
  • Classroom. In the classroom you find yourself in, look up and to the right, and you'll find him above the ceiling.
  • Gym. Look to the left and he's on the stands.
  • Bio Lab. Hiding under the desk.
  • Teacher's Lounge. Look to the right and he's under the metal covering.
  • Laundry Room. Look to the right, and he's next to the machine.
