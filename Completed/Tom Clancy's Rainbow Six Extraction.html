Quick Overview

Rainbow Six Extraction is a 3 player coop spin off. Operators from Rainbow Six Siege are called to form REACT and stop the Archaens, an unknown parasitic alien.

Note: Some trophies Autopop between versions if you choose to use your online save and there is cross progression. Some require you to repeat the action once, ie. Vertigo.

Vertigo

You can use Ela's Grzmot ability for this but she only starts with 3. The quicker and better option is to develop stun grenades which are available to any operator should Ela go MIA. You can initially carry 3, use React Tech to get more. Additionally you can develop the explosive harness so that you can start with 6.

Boom

Dissected

Take downs are performed with R3. Shooting nests does not count.

Defused

Bloaters and Breachers look like they have a orange or green nest on their back, they rush you and explode. In order to take them down use smokes or stuns to stop them from exploding and quickly run up and press R3. You can also use Vigil's ability.

Eeeeeeeew

Impact grenades are the easiest option. Studies look like small piles of Goo and often hang from the ceiling.

Clear 'Em Out

Grunts are the most common enemy so you will get this in no time.

Don't Talk to Rangers

Spikers are dangerous as they shoot spikes at you fairly rapidly and can drain your health quickly. Tormentors won't be found until San Francisco but are much more common in Alaska and are difficult to kill without stunning them.

Tidying Up

These look like tiny yellow nests and appear in clusters. Try not to get too close they will stick to you then detonate after a short period blinding and damaging you. Your teammates can destroy them if they get stuck to you.

No Hugs

Rooters are uncommon and will dig into the ground sending a trap towards you to trap you.

Defeated

You will starting encountering these in San Francisco outside of Capture the Target, but are much more common in Alaska, use a stun grenade and take them down with R3. You can also use Vigil's ability.

Regicide

You will only find these is Alaska and Truth and Consequences.

Reaper

These enemies crawl on all 4 and place blinding land mines. When shot they tend to run and hide.

Adrenaline

Hold circle to go prone and kill an enemy.

Dark Reflection

Proteans are found in singularities which can be found in San Francisco. They are a boss fight. They copy the ability and appearance of React Operators, killing them will award you a legendary charm specific to which Operator they were copying.

Welcome Home

In order to rescue one of your operator's they must go MIA (Missing in Action). Operators go MIA when they fail to extract or are not rescued by their teammates when they KO. Return to the hot zone they are in, pull them out of the archaen tree by destroying the flower like anchors, then carry them to the LZ and place them in the container.

Highly recommend doing this solo on Moderate as randoms are highly unreliable. And the difficulty is heavily reduced.

It's All Connected

Studies are specific actions you are requested to perform in a mission in order to progress research on the Archaens. You can check available studies by accessing the region menu and checking studies below the quick play button. Your current studies can also be checked in a mission by pressing options.
